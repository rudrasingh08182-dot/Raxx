### Table 7.4: Structures of Oxides of Nitrogen

Lewis dot main resonance structures and bond parameters of oxides are given in Table 7.4.

| Name | Formula | Preparation | Properties |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dinitrogen trioxide | $N_2O_3$ | $NO + NO_2 \xrightarrow{250 K} N_2O_3$ | blue solid, acidic [Nitrogen(III) oxide] |
| Nitrogen dioxide | $NO_2$ | $2Pb(NO_3)_2 \xrightarrow{673 K} 4NO_2 + 2PbO + O_2$ | brown gas, acidic [Nitrogen(IV) oxide] |
| Dinitrogen tetroxide | $N_2O_4$ | $2NO_2 \xrightleftharpoons[Heat]{Cool} N_2O_4$ | colourless solid/liquid, acidic [Nitrogen(IV) oxide] |
| Dinitrogen pentoxide | $N_2O_5$ | $4HNO_3 + P_4O_{10} \rightarrow 4HPO_3 + 2N_2O_5$ | colourless solid, acidic [Nitrogen(V) oxide] |
