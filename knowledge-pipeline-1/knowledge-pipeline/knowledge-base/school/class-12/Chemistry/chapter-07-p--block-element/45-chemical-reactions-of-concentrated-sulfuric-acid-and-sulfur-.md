What happens when:
(i) Concentrated $H_2SO_4$ is added to calcium fluoride
(ii) $SO_3$ is passed through water?

(i) It forms hydrogen fluoride:
$CaF_2 + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow CaSO_4 + 2HF$

(ii) It dissolves $SO_3$ to give $H_2SO_4$:
$SO_3 + H_2O \rightarrow H_2SO_4$
