The ionisation of $HSO_4^-$ to $H_3O^+$ and $SO_4^{2-}$ is very very small. That is why $K_{a2} \ll K_{a1}$.

7.31 In general, interhalogen compounds are more reactive than halogens due to weaker $X-X'$ bonding than $X-X$ bond. Thus, $ICl$ is more reactive than $I_2$.

7.34 Radon is radioactive with very short half-life which makes the study of chemistry of radon difficult.
