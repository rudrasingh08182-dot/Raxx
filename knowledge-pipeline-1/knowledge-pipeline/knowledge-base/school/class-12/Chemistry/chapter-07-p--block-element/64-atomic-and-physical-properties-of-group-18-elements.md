Only a very small amount of Og has been produced. Its half-life is 0.7 milliseconds. Therefore, mainly predictions about its chemistry have been made. Here, except for oganesson, important atomic and physical properties of other elements of Group 18 along with their electronic configurations are given in Table 7.12. The trends in some of the atomic, physical and chemical properties of the group are discussed here.
