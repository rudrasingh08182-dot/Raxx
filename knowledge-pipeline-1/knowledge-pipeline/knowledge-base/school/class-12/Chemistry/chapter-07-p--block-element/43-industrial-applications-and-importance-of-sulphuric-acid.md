A nation’s industrial strength can be judged by the quantity of sulphuric acid it produces and consumes. It is needed for the manufacture of hundreds of other compounds and also in many industrial processes. The bulk of sulphuric acid produced is used in the manufacture of fertilisers (e.g., ammonium sulphate, superphosphate). Other uses are in:

(a) petroleum refining
(b) manufacture of pigments, paints and dyestuff intermediates
(c) detergent industry
(d) metallurgical applications (e.g., cleansing metals before enameling, electroplating and galvanising)
(e) storage batteries
(f) in the manufacture of nitrocellulose products
(g) as a laboratory reagent.
