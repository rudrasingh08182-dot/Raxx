White phosphorus is a translucent white waxy solid. It is poisonous, insoluble in water but soluble in carbon disulphide and glows in dark (chemiluminescence). It dissolves in boiling NaOH solution in an inert atmosphere giving $PH_3$. The reaction is:

$P_4 + 3NaOH + 3H_2O \rightarrow PH_3 + 3NaH_2PO_2$

(sodium hypophosphite)
