An alcohol contains one or more hydroxyl (-OH) group(s) directly attached to carbon atom(s) of an aliphatic system ($CH_3OH$), while a phenol contains -OH group(s) directly attached to carbon atom(s) of an aromatic system ($C_6H_5OH$). The substitution of a hydrogen atom in a hydrocarbon by an alkoxy or aryloxy group ($R-O/Ar-O$) yields another class of compounds known as ‘ethers’, for example, $CH_3OCH_3$ (dimethyl ether). You may also visualise ethers as compounds formed by 2020-21.
