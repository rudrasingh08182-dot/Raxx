### Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

Allylic and benzylic alcohols may be primary, secondary or tertiary.

Vinylic alcohol: $CH_2 = CH – OH$

### 11.1.3 Ethers

(i) $CH_3 - C(CH_3)_2 - CH_2OH$
(ii) $CH_3 - CH_2 - CH_2 - CH_2OH$
(iii) $CH_3 - CH(OH) - CH_3$
(iv) $CH_3 - CH_2 - CH_2 - OH$
(v) $CH_2 = CH - CH_2OH$
(vi) $CH_3 - C(CH_3) = CH - CH_3$

### 11.2 Nomenclature
(a) Alcohols: The common name of an alcohol is derived from the common name of the alkyl group and adding the word alcohol to it. For example, $CH_3OH$ is methyl alcohol.

### 11.1.2 Phenols—Mono, Di and trihydric phenols

Ethers are classified as simple or symmetrical, if the alkyl or aryl groups attached to the oxygen atom are the same, and mixed or unsymmetrical, if the two groups are different. Diethyl ether, $C_2H_5OC_2H_5$, is a symmetrical ether whereas $C_2H_5OCH_3$ and $C_2H_5OC_6H_5$ are unsymmetrical ethers.
