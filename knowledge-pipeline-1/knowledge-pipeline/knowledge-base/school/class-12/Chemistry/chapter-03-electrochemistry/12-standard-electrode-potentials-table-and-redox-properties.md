*   Ions are present as aqueous species and $H_2O$ as liquid; gases and solids are shown by (g) and (s).
*   Increasing strength of oxidising agent (top to bottom).
*   Increasing strength of reducing agent (bottom to top).

1. A negative $E^\ominus$ means that the redox couple is a stronger reducing agent than the $H^+/H_2$ couple.
2. A positive $E^\ominus$ means that the redox couple is a weaker reducing agent than the $H^+/H_2$ couple.
