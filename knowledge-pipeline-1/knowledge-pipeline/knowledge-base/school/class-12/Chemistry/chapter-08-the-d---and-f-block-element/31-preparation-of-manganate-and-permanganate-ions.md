Fused with KOH, oxidise with air or $KNO_3$:
$2MnO_2 + 4OH^- + O_2 \rightarrow 2MnO_4^{2-} + 2H_2O$ (manganate ion)

Electrolytic oxidation in alkaline solution:
$MnO_4^{2-} \rightarrow MnO_4^-$ (manganate to permanganate ion)
