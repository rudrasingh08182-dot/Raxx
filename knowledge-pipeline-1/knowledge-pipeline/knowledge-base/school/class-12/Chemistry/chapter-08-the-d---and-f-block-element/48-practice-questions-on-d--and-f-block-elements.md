241 The d- and f- Block Elements

Occasionally. The chemistry of the actinoids is more complex in view of their ability to exist in different oxidation states. Furthermore, many of the actinoid elements are radioactive which make the study of these elements rather difficult. There are many useful applications of the d- and f-block elements and their compounds, notable among them being in varieties of steels, catalysts, complexes, organic syntheses, etc.

(i) $Cr^{3+}$ (iii) $Cu^{+}$ (v) $Co^{2+}$ (vii) $Mn^{2+}$
(ii) $Pm^{3+}$ (iv) $Ce^{4+}$ (vi) $Lu^{2+}$ (viii) $Th^{4+}$
