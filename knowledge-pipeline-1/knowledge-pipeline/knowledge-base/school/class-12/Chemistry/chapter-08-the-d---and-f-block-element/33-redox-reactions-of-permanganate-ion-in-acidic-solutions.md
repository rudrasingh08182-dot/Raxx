In acid solutions:

(a) Iodine is liberated from potassium iodide:
$10I^{-} + 2MnO_{4}^{-} + 16H^{+} \rightarrow 2Mn^{2+} + 8H_{2}O + 5I_{2}$

(b) $Fe^{2+}$ ion (green) is converted to $Fe^{3+}$ (yellow):
$5Fe^{2+} + MnO_{4}^{-} + 8H^{+} \rightarrow Mn^{2+} + 4H_{2}O + 5Fe^{3+}$
