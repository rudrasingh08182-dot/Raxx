### 14.2 Amino Acids

The general structure of amino acids is represented as:

$R-CH(NH_2)-COOH$

(where $R$ = side chain)

Alternatively represented as:

$H_2N-CH(R)-COOH$

#### Table 14.2: Natural Amino Acids

| Name of the amino acids | Characteristic feature of side chain, R | Three letter symbol | One letter code |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Leucine | $(H_3C)_2CH-CH_2-$ | Leu | L |
