### Polymers of Commercial Importance

Polymers are defined as high molecular mass macromolecules, which consist of repeating structural units derived from the corresponding monomers. These polymers may be of natural or synthetic origin and are classified in a number of ways.

In the presence of an organic peroxide initiator, the alkenes and their derivatives undergo addition polymerisation or chain growth polymerisation through a free radical mechanism. Polythene, teflon, orlon, etc. are formed by addition polymerisation of an appropriate alkene or its derivative. Condensation polymerisation reactions are shown by the interaction of bi- or poly functional monomers containing $-NH_2$, $-OH$ and $-COOH$ groups. This type of polymerisation proceeds through the elimination of certain simple molecules as $H_2O$, $CH_3OH$, etc. Formaldehyde reacts with phenol and melamine to form the corresponding condensation polymer products.
