Electron withdrawing groups increase the acidity of carboxylic acids by stabilising the conjugate base through delocalisation of the negative charge by inductive and/or resonance effects. Conversely, electron donating groups decrease the acidity by destabilising the conjugate base.

- Electron withdrawing group (EWG) stabilises the carboxylate anion and strengthens the acid.
- Electron donating group (EDG) destabilises the carboxylate anion and weakens the acid.
