It is obvious from equation (2.11) that higher the value of $K_H$ at a given pressure, the lower is the solubility of the gas in the liquid. It can be seen from Table 2.2 that $K_H$ values for both $N_2$ and $O_2$ increase with increase of temperature indicating that the solubility of gases decreases with increase of temperature.
