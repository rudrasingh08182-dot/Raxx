For example, if we dissolve one mole of KCl (74.5 g) in water, we expect one mole each of $K^+$ and $Cl^–$ ions to be released in the solution. If this happens, there would be two moles of particles in the solution. If we ignore interionic attractions, one mole of KCl in one kg of water would be expected to increase the boiling point by $2 \times 0.52 \text{ K} = 1.04 \text{ K}$.
