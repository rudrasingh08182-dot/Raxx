The equation (2.20) given in Section 2.4.3 establishes a relation between vapour pressure of the solution, mole fraction and vapour pressure of the solvent, i.e., $p_1 = x_1 p_1^0$ (2.22). The reduction in the vapour pressure of solvent ($\Delta p_1$) is given as: $\Delta p_1 = p_1^0 - p_1 = p_1^0 - p_1^0 x_1 = p_1^0 (1 - x_1)$ (2.23). Knowing that $x_2 = 1 - x_1$, equation (2.23) reduces to $\Delta p_1 = x_2 p_1^0$ (2.24). In a solution containing several non-volatile solutes, the lowering of the vapour pressure depends on the sum of the mole fraction of different solutes. Equation (2.24) can be written as $\frac{p_1^0 - p_1}{p_1^0} = x_2$ (2.25).
