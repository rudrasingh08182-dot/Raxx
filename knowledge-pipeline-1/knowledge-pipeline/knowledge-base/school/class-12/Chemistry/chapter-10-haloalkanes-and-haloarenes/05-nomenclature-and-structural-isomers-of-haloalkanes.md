Gem-dihalides are compounds where halogen atoms are present on the same carbon atom, and vicinal halides or vic-dihalides are compounds when halogen atoms are present on adjacent carbon atoms. In the common name system, gem-dihalides are named as alkylidene halides and vic-dihalides are named as alkylene dihalides. In the IUPAC system, they are named as dihaloalkanes.

Example 10.1
Solution: Draw the structures of all the eight structural isomers that have the molecular formula $C_5H_{11}Br$. Name each isomer according to IUPAC system and classify them as primary, secondary or tertiary bromide.

1. $CH_3CH_2CH_2CH_2CH_2Br$ : 1-Bromopentane ($1^o$)
2. $CH_3CH_2CH_2CH(Br)CH_3$ : 2-Bromopentane ($2^o$)
3. $CH_3CH_2CH(Br)CH_2CH_3$ : 3-Bromopentane ($2^o$)
4. $(CH_3)_2CHCH_2CH_2Br$ : 1-Bromo-3-methylbutane ($1^o$)
