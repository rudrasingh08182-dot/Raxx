### Table 9.1: Formulation of Cobalt(III) Chloride-Ammonia Complexes

| Colour | Formula | Solution conductivity | Electrolyte Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Yellow | $[Co(NH_3)_6]^{3+} 3Cl^-$ | $3Cl^-$ | 1:3 electrolyte |
| Purple | $[CoCl(NH_3)_5]^{2+} 2Cl^-$ | $2Cl^-$ | 1:2 electrolyte |
| Green | $[CoCl_2(NH_3)_4]^+ Cl^-$ | $Cl^-$ | 1:1 electrolyte |
| Violet | $[CoCl_2(NH_3)_4]^+ Cl^-$ | $Cl^-$ | 1:1 electrolyte |
