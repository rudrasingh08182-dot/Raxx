In $[Ni(NH_3)_6]^{2+}$, Ni is in +2 oxidation state and has $d^8$ configuration, the hybridisation involved is $sp^3d^2$ forming outer orbital complex. For square planar shape, the hybridisation is $dsp^2$. Hence the unpaired electrons in $5d$ orbital pair up to make one $d$ orbital empty for $dsp^2$ hybridisation. Thus there is no unpaired electron.
