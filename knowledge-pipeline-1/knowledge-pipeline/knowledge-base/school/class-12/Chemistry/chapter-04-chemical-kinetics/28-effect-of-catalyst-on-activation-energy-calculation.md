### 4.11: Effect of catalyst on activation energy

$\log \frac{k_2}{k_1} = \frac{E_a}{2.303 R} \left[ \frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2} \right]$

$\log \frac{k_2}{k_1} = \frac{209000 \text{ J mol}^{-1}}{2.303 \times 8.314 \text{ J mol}^{-1} \text{ K}^{-1}} \left[ \frac{1}{600 \text{ K}} - \frac{1}{700 \text{ K}} \right]$

$\log \frac{k_2}{1.60 \times 10^{-5}} = -4.796 + 2.599 = -2.197$

$k_2 = 6.36 \times 10^{-3} \text{ s}^{-1}$
