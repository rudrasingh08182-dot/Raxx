4.10 In a reaction between A and B, the initial rate of reaction ($r_0$) was measured for different initial concentrations of A and B as given below:

| A/ mol L⁻¹ | B/ mol L⁻¹ | $r_0$/mol L⁻¹s⁻¹ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0.20 | 0.30 | 5.07 × 10⁻⁵ |
| 0.20 | 0.10 | 5.07 × 10⁻⁵ |
| 0.40 | 0.05 | 1.43 × 10⁻⁴ |

What is the order of the reaction with respect to A and B?
