If $[R]_1$ and $[P]_1$ are the concentrations of R and P respectively at time $t_1$ and $[R]_2$ and $[P]_2$ are their concentrations at time $t_2$ then,

$\Delta t = t_2 - t_1$
$\Delta [R] = [R]_2 - [R]_1$
$\Delta [P] = [P]_2 - [P]_1$

The square brackets in the above expressions are used to express molar concentration. Rate of disappearance of R:

Rate $= -\frac{\Delta [R]}{\Delta t}$ (4.1)
