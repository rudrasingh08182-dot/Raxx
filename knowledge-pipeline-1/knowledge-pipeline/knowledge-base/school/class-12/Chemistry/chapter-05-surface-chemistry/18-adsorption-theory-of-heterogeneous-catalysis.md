### 5.2.2 Adsorption Theory of Heterogeneous Catalysis

When a gas comes in contact with such a surface, its molecules are held up there due to loose chemical combination. If different molecules are adsorbed side by side, they may react with each other resulting in the formation of new molecules. Thus, formed molecules may evaporate leaving the surface for the fresh reactant molecules. This theory explains why the catalyst remains unchanged in mass and chemical composition at the end of the reaction and is effective.
