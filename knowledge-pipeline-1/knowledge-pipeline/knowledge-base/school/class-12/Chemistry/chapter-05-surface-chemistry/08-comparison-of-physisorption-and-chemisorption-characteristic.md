More easily liquefiable gases are adsorbed readily. Enthalpy of adsorption is low ($20-40 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$) in this case. Chemisorption characteristics:

1. It is caused by chemical bond formation.
2. It is highly specific in nature.
3. It is irreversible.
4. It also depends on the nature of gas. Gases which can react with the adsorbent show chemisorption.
5. Enthalpy of adsorption is high ($80-240 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$) in this case.
