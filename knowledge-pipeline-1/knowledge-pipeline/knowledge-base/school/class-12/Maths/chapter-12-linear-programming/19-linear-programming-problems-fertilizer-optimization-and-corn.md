There are two types of fertilisers $F_1$ and $F_2$. $F_1$ consists of 10% nitrogen and 6% phosphoric acid and $F_2$ consists of 5% nitrogen and 10% phosphoric acid. After testing the soil conditions, a farmer finds that she needs at least 14 kg of nitrogen and 14 kg of phosphoric acid for her crop. If $F_1$ costs Rs 6/kg and $F_2$ costs Rs 5/kg, determine how much of each type of fertiliser should be used so that nutrient requirements are met at a minimum cost. What is the minimum cost?
