19. The area bounded by the y-axis, y = cos x and y = sin x when 0 ≤ x ≤ π/2 is
(A) 2(√2 – 1) (B) √2 – 1 (C) √2 + 1 (D) √2

Summary

* The area of the region bounded by the curve y = f(x), x-axis and the lines x = a and x = b (b > a) is given by the formula: $\text{Area} = \int_{a}^{b} y dx = \int_{a}^{b} f(x) dx$.

* The area of the region bounded by the curve x = φ(y), y-axis and the lines y = c, y = d is given by the formula: $\text{Area} = \int_{c}^{d} x dy = \int_{c}^{d} φ(y) dy$.
