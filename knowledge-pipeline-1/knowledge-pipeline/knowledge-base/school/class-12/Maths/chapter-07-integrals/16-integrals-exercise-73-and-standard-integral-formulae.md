### 7.4 Integrals of Some Particular Functions

In this section, we mention below some important formulae of integrals and apply them for integrating many other related standard integrals:

(1) $\int \frac{dx}{x^2 - a^2} = \frac{1}{2a} \log \left| \frac{x-a}{x+a} \right| + C$

(2) $\int \frac{dx}{a^2 - x^2} = \frac{1}{2a} \log \left| \frac{a+x}{a-x} \right| + C$

(3) $\int \frac{dx}{x^2 + a^2} = \frac{1}{a} \tan^{-1} \frac{x}{a} + C$

(4) $\int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}} = \log \left| x + \sqrt{x^2 - a^2} \right| + C$

(5) $\int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} = \sin^{-1} \frac{x}{a} + C$

(6) $\int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 + a^2}} = \log \left| x + \sqrt{x^2 + a^2} \right| + C$

We now prove the above results:

(1) We have $\frac{1}{x^2 - a^2} = \frac{1}{(x-a)(x+a)} = \frac{1}{2a} \left[ \frac{1}{x-a} - \frac{1}{x+a} \right]$

Therefore, $\int \frac{dx}{x^2 - a^2} = \frac{1}{2a} \left[ \int \frac{dx}{x-a} - \int \frac{dx}{x+a} \right]$
$= \frac{1}{2a} [ \log |x-a| - \log |x+a| ] + C = \frac{1}{2a} \log \left| \frac{x-a}{x+a} \right| + C$

(2) In view of (1) above, we have $\frac{1}{a^2 - x^2} = \frac{1}{(a-x)(a+x)} = \frac{1}{2a} \left[ \frac{1}{a+x} + \frac{1}{a-x} \right]$
