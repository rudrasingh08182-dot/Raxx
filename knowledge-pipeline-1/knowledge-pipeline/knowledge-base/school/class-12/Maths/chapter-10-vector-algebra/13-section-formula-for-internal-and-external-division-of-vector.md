### VECTOR ALGEBRA 439

**Case II: When R divides PQ externally (Fig 10.17).**
We leave it to the reader as an exercise to verify that the position vector of the point R which divides the line segment PQ externally in the ratio $m:n$ (i.e., $\frac{PR}{QR} = \frac{m}{n}$) is given by $\vec{r} = \frac{m\vec{b} - n\vec{a}}{m - n}$.

**Remark:** If R is the midpoint of PQ, then $m = n$. And therefore, from Case I, the midpoint R of PQ will have its position vector as $\vec{r} = \frac{\vec{a} + \vec{b}}{2}$.

**Example 11:** Consider two points P and Q with position vectors $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$. Find the position vector of a point R which divides the line joining P and Q in the ratio 2:1, (i) internally, and (ii) externally.

**Solution:**
(i) The position vector of the point R dividing the join of P and Q internally in the ratio 2:1 is $\vec{r} = \frac{2\vec{b} + 1\vec{a}}{2 + 1} = \frac{2\vec{b} + \vec{a}}{3}$.
(ii) The position vector of the point R dividing the join of P and Q externally in the ratio 2:1 is $\vec{r} = \frac{2\vec{b} - 1\vec{a}}{2 - 1} = 2\vec{b} - \vec{a}$.

**Example 12:** Show that the points $A(2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k})$, $B(3\hat{i} - 5\hat{j} + 4\hat{k})$, $C(3\hat{i} - 4\hat{j} - 4\hat{k})$ are the vertices of a right-angled triangle.

**Solution:** We have
$\vec{AB} = (3-2)\hat{i} + (-5+1)\hat{j} + (4-1)\hat{k} = \hat{i} - 4\hat{j} + 3\hat{k}$
$\vec{BC} = (3-3)\hat{i} + (-4+5)\hat{j} + (-4-4)\hat{k} = \hat{j} - 8\hat{k}$
$\vec{CA} = (2-3)\hat{i} + (-1+4)\hat{j} + (1+4)\hat{k} = -\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + 5\hat{k}$
