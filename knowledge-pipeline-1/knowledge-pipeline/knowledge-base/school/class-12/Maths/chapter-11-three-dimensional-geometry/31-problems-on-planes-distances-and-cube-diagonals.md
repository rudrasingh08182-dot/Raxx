Miscellaneous Examples

Example 26: A line makes angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ and $\delta$ with the diagonals of a cube, prove that $\cos^2 \alpha + \cos^2 \beta + \cos^2 \gamma + \cos^2 \delta = \frac{4}{3}$.

Solution: A cube is a rectangular parallelopiped having equal length, breadth and height. Let OADBFEGC be the cube with each side of length $a$ units. (Fig 11.21). The four diagonals are OE, AF, BG and CD. The direction cosines of the diagonal OE which is the line joining two points O and E are $\frac{a-0}{\sqrt{a^2+a^2+a^2}}, \frac{a-0}{\sqrt{a^2+a^2+a^2}}, \frac{a-0}{\sqrt{a^2+a^2+a^2}}$ i.e., $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$.
