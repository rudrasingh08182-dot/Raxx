In this regard, the parents and the teachers have a special responsibility. Parenting that combines high levels of nurturance and consistent discipline has been associated with a lowered risk of substance (alcohol/drugs/tobacco) abuse. Some of the measures mentioned here would be particularly useful for the prevention and control of alcohol and drug abuse among adolescents:

(i) Avoid undue peer pressure - Every child has his/her own choice and personality, which should be respected and nurtured. A child should not be pushed unduly to perform beyond his/her threshold limits; be it studies, sports, or other activities.
