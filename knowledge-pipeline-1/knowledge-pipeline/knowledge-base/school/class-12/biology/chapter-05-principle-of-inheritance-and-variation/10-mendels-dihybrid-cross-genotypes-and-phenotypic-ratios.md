Let us use the genotypic symbols Y for dominant yellow seed colour and y for recessive green seed colour, R for round shaped seeds and r for wrinkled seed shape. The genotype of the parents can then be written as $RRYY$ and $rryy$. The cross between the two plants can be written down as in Figure 5.7 showing the genotypes of the parent plants. The gametes $RY$ and $ry$ unite on fertilisation to produce the $F_1$ hybrid $RrYy$. When Mendel self hybridised the $F_1$ plants he found that 3/4th of $F_2$ plants had yellow seeds and 1/4th had green. The yellow and green colour segregated in a 3:1 ratio. Round and wrinkled seed shape also segregated in a 3:1 ratio; just like in a monohybrid cross.
