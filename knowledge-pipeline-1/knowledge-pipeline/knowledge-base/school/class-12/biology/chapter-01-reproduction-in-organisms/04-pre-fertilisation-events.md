Pre-fertilisation events include gametogenesis (formation of gametes) and gamete transfer. Gametes are haploid cells. In some algae, gametes are similar (homogametes), but most organisms produce morphologically distinct male and female gametes (heterogametes). In diploid organisms, specialised cells called meiocytes undergo meiosis to produce haploid gametes. Following formation, gametes must be brought together. In many simple plants, water acts as the medium for transfer. In seed plants, pollen grains carry male gametes to the stigma via pollination.
