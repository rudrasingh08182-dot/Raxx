Biology is the story of life on Earth. While individual organisms die, species continue to exist through reproduction. Reproduction is a vital biological process where an organism gives rise to young ones (offspring) similar to itself, ensuring the continuity of the species generation after generation. The period from birth to the natural death of an organism represents its life span. Life spans vary significantly among organisms, ranging from a few days to several thousand years, and are not necessarily correlated with body size. Death of every individual organism is a certainty, except for single-celled organisms, which are considered immortal in the sense that they do not undergo natural death.
