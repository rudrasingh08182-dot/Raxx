The fusion of male and female gametes is called syngamy or fertilisation, resulting in a diploid zygote. If syngamy occurs outside the body, it is external fertilisation (e.g., fish, amphibians); if inside, it is internal fertilisation (e.g., reptiles, birds, mammals, most plants). Post-fertilisation events include the development of the zygote into an embryo (embryogenesis). Animals are classified as oviparous (egg-laying) or viviparous (giving birth to young). In flowering plants, the zygote develops into an embryo, the ovule becomes a seed, and the ovary develops into a fruit.
