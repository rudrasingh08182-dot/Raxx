In this case, one antibiotic resistance gene helps in selecting the transformants, whereas the other antibiotic resistance gene is used for insertion. Figure 11.4 shows the E. coli cloning vector pBR322, which includes restriction sites (Hind III, EcoR I, BamH I, Sal I, Pvu II, Pst I, Cla I), ori, and antibiotic resistance genes ($amp^R$ and $tet^R$). The rop gene codes for the proteins involved in the replication of the plasmid.
