Niels Henrik David Bohr (1885 – 1962) was a Danish physicist who explained the spectrum of the hydrogen atom based on quantum ideas. He gave a theory of nuclear fission based on the liquid-drop model of the nucleus. Bohr contributed to the clarification of conceptual problems in quantum mechanics, in particular by proposing the complementary principle.
