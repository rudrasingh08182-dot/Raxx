6. In Bohr model, contrary to ordinary classical expectation, the frequency of revolution of an electron in its orbit is not connected to the frequency of spectral line. The latter is the difference between two orbital energies divided by $h$.

For transitions between large quantum numbers ($n$ to $n - 1$, $n$ very large), however, the two coincide as expected.
