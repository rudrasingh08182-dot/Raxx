The electrostatic force of attraction, $F_e$, between the revolving electrons and the nucleus provides the requisite centripetal force ($F_c$) to keep them in their orbits. Thus, for a dynamically stable orbit in a hydrogen atom:

$F_e = F_c$

$\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \frac{e^2}{r^2} = \frac{mv^2}{r}$ (12.2)
