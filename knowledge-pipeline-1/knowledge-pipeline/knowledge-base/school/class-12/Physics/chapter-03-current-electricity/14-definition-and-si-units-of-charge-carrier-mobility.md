An important quantity is the mobility $\mu$ defined as the magnitude of the drift velocity per unit electric field:

$$\mu = \frac{|v_d|}{E}$$

(3.24)

The SI unit of mobility is $m^2/Vs$ and is $10^4$ of the mobility in practical units ($cm^2/Vs$). Mobility is positive. From Eq. (3.17), we have

$$v_d = \frac{eE\tau}{m}$$
