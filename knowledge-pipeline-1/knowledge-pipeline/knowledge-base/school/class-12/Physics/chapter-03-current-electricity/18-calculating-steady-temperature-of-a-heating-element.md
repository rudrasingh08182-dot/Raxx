The resistance $R_2$ at the steady temperature $T_2$ is
$R_2 = \frac{230 \text{ V}}{2.68 \text{ A}} = 85.8 \text{ } \Omega$

Using the relation
$R_2 = R_1 [1 + \alpha (T_2 - T_1)]$
with $\alpha = 1.70 \times 10^{-4} \text{ °C}^{-1}$, we get
$T_2 - T_1 = \frac{(85.8 - 75.3)}{(75.3) \times 1.70 \times 10^{-4}}$
$= 820 \text{ °C}$

that is, $T_2 = (820 + 27.0) \text{ °C} = 847 \text{ °C}$

Thus, the steady temperature of the heating element (when heating effect due to the current equals heat loss to the surroundings) is 847 °C.
