In practical calculations, internal resistances of cells in the circuit may be neglected when the current $I$ is such that $\epsilon \gg Ir$. The actual values of the internal resistances of cells vary from cell to cell. The internal resistance of dry cells, however, is much higher than the common electrolytic cells.
