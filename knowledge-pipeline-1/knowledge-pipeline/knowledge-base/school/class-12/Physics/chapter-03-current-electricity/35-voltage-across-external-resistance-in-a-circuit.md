A rectifier combines features (a) and (b). GaAs shows the feature (c). 11. When a source of emf $\epsilon$ is connected to an external resistance $R$, the voltage $V_{ext}$ across $R$ is given by $V_{ext} = IR = \frac{R}{R+r} \epsilon$, where $r$ is the internal resistance of the source.
