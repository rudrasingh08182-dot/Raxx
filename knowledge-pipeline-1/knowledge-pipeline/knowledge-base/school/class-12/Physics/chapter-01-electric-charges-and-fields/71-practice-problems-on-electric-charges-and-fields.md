1.21 A uniformly charged conducting sphere of 2.4 m diameter has a surface charge density of $80.0 \mu C/m^2$. (a) Find the charge on the sphere. (b) What is the total electric flux leaving the surface of the sphere?

1.22 An infinite line charge produces a field of $9 \times 10^4 \text{ N/C}$ at a distance of 2 cm. Calculate the linear charge density.

1.23 Two large, thin metal plates are parallel and close to each other. On their inner faces, the plates have surface charge densities of opposite signs and of magnitude $17.0 \times 10^{-22} \text{ C/m}^2$. What is E: (a) in the outer region of the first plate, (b) in the outer region of the second plate, and (c) between the plates?
