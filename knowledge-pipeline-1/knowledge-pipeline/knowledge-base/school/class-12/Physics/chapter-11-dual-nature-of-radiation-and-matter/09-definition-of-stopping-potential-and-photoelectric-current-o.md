For a particular frequency of incident radiation, the minimum negative (retarding) potential $V_0$ given to the plate A for which the photocurrent stops or becomes zero is called the cut-off or stopping potential. The interpretation of the observation in terms of photoelectrons is straightforward. All the photoelectrons emitted from the metal do not have the same energy.
