The potential energy of a system of three charges is given by:

$U = \frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0} \left( \frac{q_1 q_2}{r_{12}} + \frac{q_1 q_3}{r_{13}} + \frac{q_2 q_3}{r_{23}} \right)$ (2.26)

Again, because of the conservative nature of the electrostatic force (or equivalently, the path independence of work done), the final expression for $U$, Eq. (2.26), is independent of the manner in which the configuration is assembled.
