Magnifying power $m$ of a telescope is the ratio of the angle $\beta$ subtended at the eye by the image to the angle $\alpha$ subtended at the eye by the object. 

$m = \frac{\beta}{\alpha} = \frac{f_0}{f_e}$

where $f_0$ and $f_e$ are the focal lengths of the objective and eyepiece, respectively.
