To supply a given power at a given voltage, if $\cos \phi$ is small, we have to increase current accordingly. But this will lead to large power loss ($I^2R$) in transmission. Suppose in a circuit, current $I$ lags the voltage by an angle $\phi$. Then power factor $\cos \phi = R/Z$. We can improve the power factor (tending to 1) by making $Z$ tend to $R$. Let us understand, with the help of a phasor diagram (Fig. 7.15).
