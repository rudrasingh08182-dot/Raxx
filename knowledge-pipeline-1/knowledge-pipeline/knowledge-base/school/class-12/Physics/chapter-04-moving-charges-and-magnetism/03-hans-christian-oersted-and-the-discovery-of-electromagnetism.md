Hans Christian Oersted (1777–1851) was a Danish physicist and chemist, and a professor at Copenhagen. He observed that a compass needle suffers a deflection when placed near a wire carrying an electric current. This discovery gave the first empirical evidence of a connection between electric and magnetic phenomena.
