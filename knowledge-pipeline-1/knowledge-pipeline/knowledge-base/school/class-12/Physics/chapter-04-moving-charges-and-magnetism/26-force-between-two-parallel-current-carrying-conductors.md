Figure 4.17 shows two long parallel conductors a and b separated by a distance d and carrying (parallel) currents $I_a$ and $I_b$, respectively. The conductor ‘a’ produces the same magnetic field $B_a$ at all points along the conductor ‘b’. The right-hand rule tells us that the direction of this field is downwards (when the conductors are placed horizontally). Its magnitude is given by Eq. [4.15(a)] or from Ampere’s circuital law,

$B_a = \frac{\mu_0 I_a}{2 \pi d}$

The conductor ‘b’ carrying a current $I_b$ will experience a sideways force due to the field $B_a$. The direction of this force is towards the conductor ‘a’ (Verify this). We label this force as $F_{ba}$, the force on a segment L of ‘b’ due to ‘a’. The magnitude of this force is given by Eq. (4.4),

$F_{ba} = I_b L B_a$
