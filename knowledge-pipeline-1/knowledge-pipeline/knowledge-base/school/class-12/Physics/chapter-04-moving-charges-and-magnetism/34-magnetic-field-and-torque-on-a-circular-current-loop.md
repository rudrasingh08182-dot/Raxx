### Example 4.10

**Solution**

(a) From Eq. (4.12)
$B = \frac{\mu_0 N I}{2R}$

Here, $N = 100$; $I = 3.2 \text{ A}$, and $R = 0.1 \text{ m}$. Hence,
$B = \frac{4 \times 10^{-7} \times 100 \times 3.2}{2 \times 0.1} = 2 \times 10^{-3} \text{ T}$ (using $\pi \times 3.2 \approx 10$)

The direction is given by the right-hand thumb rule.

(b) The magnetic moment is given by Eq. (4.24),
$m = N I A = N I \pi r^2 = 100 \times 3.2 \times 3.14 \times 10^{-2} = 10 \text{ A m}^2$

The direction is once again given by the right-hand thumb rule.

(c) $\tau = m \times B$ [from Eq. (4.23)]
$= m B \sin \theta$

Initially, $\theta = 0$. Thus, initial torque $\tau_i = 0$. Finally, $\theta = \pi/2$ (or $90^\circ$).
Thus, final torque $\tau_f = m B = 10 \times 2 = 20 \text{ N m}$.

$I \alpha = \tau$
