**Example 1:** Find the zeroes of $x^2 + 7x + 10$ and verify the relationship.
- Factorization: $(x + 2)(x + 5) = 0$. Zeroes are $-2$ and $-5$.
- Sum: $-2 + (-5) = -7 = -\frac{7}{1}$. (Verified)
- Product: $(-2) \times (-5) = 10 = \frac{10}{1}$. (Verified)

**Example 2:** Find a quadratic polynomial with sum of zeroes $-3$ and product $2$.
- $\alpha + \beta = -3 = -\frac{b}{a}$ and $\alpha\beta = 2 = \frac{c}{a}$.
- Taking $a=1$, we get $b=3$ and $c=2$. The polynomial is $x^2 + 3x + 2$.
