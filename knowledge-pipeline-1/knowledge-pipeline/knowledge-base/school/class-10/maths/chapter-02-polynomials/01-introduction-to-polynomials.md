In Class IX, you studied polynomials in one variable and their degrees. If $p(x)$ is a polynomial in $x$, the highest power of $x$ in $p(x)$ is called the degree of the polynomial. 

- **Linear Polynomial:** A polynomial of degree 1 (e.g., $2x - 3$).
- **Quadratic Polynomial:** A polynomial of degree 2. The general form is $ax^2 + bx + c$, where $a, b, c$ are real numbers and $a \neq 0$.
- **Cubic Polynomial:** A polynomial of degree 3. The general form is $ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$, where $a, b, c, d$ are real numbers and $a \neq 0$.

Expressions like $\frac{1}{x-1}$ or $\sqrt{x} + 2$ are not polynomials. The value of a polynomial $p(x)$ at $x=k$ is denoted by $p(k)$. A real number $k$ is a zero of $p(x)$ if $p(k) = 0$.
