1. Find the number of zeroes for the given graphs of $y = p(x)$ (refer to Fig 2.10 in textbook).
2. Find the zeroes of the following quadratic polynomials and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients:
   (i) $x^2 - 2x - 8$
   (ii) $4s^2 - 4s + 1$
   (iii) $6x^2 - 3 - 7x$
   (iv) $4u^2 + 8u$
   (v) $t^2 - 15$
   (vi) $3x^2 - x - 4$
3. Find a quadratic polynomial for each with the given numbers as the sum and product of its zeroes respectively:
   (i) $\frac{1}{4}, -1$ (ii) $\sqrt{2}, \frac{1}{3}$ (iii) $0, \sqrt{5}$
