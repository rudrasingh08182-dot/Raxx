### Playing Cards
A deck has 52 cards. 
- $P(\text{ace}) = 4/52 = 1/13$
- $P(\text{not an ace}) = 1 - 1/13 = 12/13$

### Two Dice Experiment
When two dice are thrown, there are $6 \times 6 = 36$ possible outcomes. 
- To find the probability that the sum is 8: The favourable outcomes are (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2). 
- $P(\text{sum is 8}) = 5/36$.
