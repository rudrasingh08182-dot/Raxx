1. Theoretical probability $P(E) = \frac{\text{Favourable outcomes}}{\text{Total outcomes}}$.
2. Probability of a sure event is 1; impossible event is 0.
3. $0 \le P(E) \le 1$.
4. Sum of probabilities of all elementary events is 1.
5. $P(E) + P(\overline{E}) = 1$.
