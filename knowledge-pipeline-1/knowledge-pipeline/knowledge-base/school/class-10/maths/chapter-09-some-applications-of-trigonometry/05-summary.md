1. **Line of Sight:** The line from the observer's eye to the object.
2. **Angle of Elevation:** Formed when looking up at an object above the horizontal level.
3. **Angle of Depression:** Formed when looking down at an object below the horizontal level.
4. **Calculation:** Heights and distances are determined using trigonometric ratios ($\sin, \cos, \tan$) in right-angled triangles.
