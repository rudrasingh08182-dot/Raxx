### Example 1
A tower stands vertically on the ground. From a point 15 m away from the foot of the tower, the angle of elevation of the top is 60°. 
**Solution:** Using $\tan 60° = \frac{AB}{BC}$, we get $\sqrt{3} = \frac{AB}{15}$, so $AB = 15\sqrt{3}$ m.

### Example 2
An electrician needs to reach a point 1.3 m below the top of a 5 m pole. Using a ladder at 60° to the horizontal, find the ladder length.
**Solution:** Height to reach $BD = 5 - 1.3 = 3.7$ m. Using $\sin 60° = \frac{BD}{BC}$, we get $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{3.7}{BC}$, so $BC \approx 4.28$ m.

### Example 3
An observer 1.5 m tall is 28.5 m away from a chimney. The angle of elevation is 45°. 
**Solution:** $\tan 45° = \frac{AE}{28.5} \implies AE = 28.5$. Total height $= 28.5 + 1.5 = 30$ m.
