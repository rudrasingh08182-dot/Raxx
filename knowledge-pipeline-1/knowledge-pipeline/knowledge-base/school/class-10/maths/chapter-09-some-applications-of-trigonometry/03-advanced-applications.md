### Example 5: Shadow Problem
The shadow of a tower is 40 m longer when the Sun's altitude is 30° than when it is 60°. 
**Solution:** Let height be $h$ and shadow be $x$. 
1. $\tan 60° = \frac{h}{x} \implies h = x\sqrt{3}$
2. $\tan 30° = \frac{h}{x+40} \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{x\sqrt{3}}{x+40}$
Solving gives $x = 20$, so $h = 20\sqrt{3}$ m.
