1. A circus artist climbs a 20 m long rope tied to a pole at 30°. Find the pole height.
2. A tree breaks at a point, touching the ground at 30°, 8 m from the foot. Find the tree height.
3. A contractor installs two slides: 1.5 m height at 30° and 3 m height at 60°. Find slide lengths.
4. A kite flies at 60 m height with string at 60° inclination. Find string length.
5. A 1.5 m tall boy observes a 30 m building; the angle of elevation changes from 30° to 60°. Find the distance walked.
6. A TV tower stands on a canal bank. From the other bank, the angle is 60°; 20 m back, it is 30°. Find tower height and canal width.
