### Theorem 10.1
**The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.**

*   **Proof Summary:** If we take a point $Q$ on the tangent $XY$ (other than the point of contact $P$), the distance $OQ$ is always greater than the radius $OP$ because $Q$ lies outside the circle. Since $OP$ is the shortest distance from the centre $O$ to the line $XY$, $OP \perp XY$.

*   **Remarks:**
    *   At any point on a circle, there is one and only one tangent.
    *   The line containing the radius through the point of contact is often called the 'normal' to the circle.
