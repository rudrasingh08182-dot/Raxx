#### Example 1
Prove that in two concentric circles, the chord of the larger circle, which touches the smaller circle, is bisected at the point of contact.
*Solution:* Let $OP$ be the radius to the point of contact $P$. Since $OP \perp AB$ (tangent to the smaller circle), and the perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord, $AP = BP$.

#### Example 2
Two tangents $TP$ and $TQ$ are drawn to a circle with centre $O$ from an external point $T$. Prove that $\angle PTQ = 2 \angle OPQ$.
*Solution:* Using the properties of isosceles triangles and the fact that $\angle OPT = 90^\circ$, we derive $\angle OPQ = 90^\circ - (90^\circ - \frac{1}{2}\theta) = \frac{1}{2}\theta$, where $\theta = \angle PTQ$.
