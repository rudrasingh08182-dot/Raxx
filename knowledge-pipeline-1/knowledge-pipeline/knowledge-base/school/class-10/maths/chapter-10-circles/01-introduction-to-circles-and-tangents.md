A circle is defined as the collection of all points in a plane that are at a constant distance (radius) from a fixed point (centre). When considering a circle and a line in the same plane, there are three possible scenarios:

1. **Non-intersecting line:** The line and the circle have no common points.
2. **Secant:** The line intersects the circle at two distinct points.
3. **Tangent:** The line intersects the circle at exactly one point. This point is called the point of contact, and the line is said to touch the circle at this point.

A tangent is a special case of a secant where the two points of intersection coincide.
