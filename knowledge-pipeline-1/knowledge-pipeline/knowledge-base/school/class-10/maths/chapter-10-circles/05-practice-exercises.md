1. How many tangents can a circle have?
2. A tangent $PQ$ at a point $P$ of a circle of radius $5\text{ cm}$ meets a line through the centre $O$ at a point $Q$ so that $OQ = 12\text{ cm}$. Find the length $PQ$.
3. Prove that the tangents drawn at the ends of a diameter of a circle are parallel.
4. The length of a tangent from a point $A$ at distance $5\text{ cm}$ from the centre of the circle is $4\text{ cm}$. Find the radius of the circle.
5. Prove that the parallelogram circumscribing a circle is a rhombus.
