Unlike surface area, where some parts disappear upon joining, the volume of a solid formed by joining two or more basic solids is simply the sum of the volumes of the individual constituent solids. 

If a solid is composed of a cuboid and a half-cylinder, the total volume is:
$V_{total} = V_{cuboid} + V_{half-cylinder}$
