Example 1: A top is shaped like a cone surmounted by a hemisphere. Given height = 5 cm and diameter = 3.5 cm. 
$TSA = CSA_{hemisphere} + CSA_{cone} = 2\pi r^2 + \pi rl$. 

Example 2: A decorative block is a cube (edge 5 cm) with a hemisphere (diameter 4.2 cm) on top. 
$Surface Area = TSA_{cube} - BaseArea_{hemisphere} + CSA_{hemisphere} = 6a^2 - \pi r^2 + 2\pi r^2 = 6a^2 + \pi r^2$.
