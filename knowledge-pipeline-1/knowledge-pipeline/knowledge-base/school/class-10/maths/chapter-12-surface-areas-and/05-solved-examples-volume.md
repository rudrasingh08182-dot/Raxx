Example 5: A shed is a cuboid (7m x 15m x 8m) surmounted by a half-cylinder (diameter 7m, height 15m). 
$Volume = (l \times b \times h) + \frac{1}{2}(\pi r^2 h)$. 

Example 6: A cylindrical glass (diameter 5 cm, height 10 cm) has a hemispherical raised portion at the bottom. 
$Actual Capacity = V_{cylinder} - V_{hemisphere}$.
