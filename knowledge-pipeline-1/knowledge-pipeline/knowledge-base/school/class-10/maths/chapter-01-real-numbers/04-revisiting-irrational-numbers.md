An irrational number is a number that cannot be written in the form $\frac{p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are integers and $q \neq 0$. 

**Theorem 1.2:** Let $p$ be a prime number. If $p$ divides $a^2$, then $p$ divides $a$, where $a$ is a positive integer.

**Theorem 1.3:** $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.
*Proof Summary:* Assume $\sqrt{2} = \frac{a}{b}$ where $a, b$ are coprime. Squaring gives $2b^2 = a^2$, implying $2$ divides $a^2$ and thus $2$ divides $a$. Substituting $a=2c$ leads to $b^2 = 2c^2$, implying $2$ divides $b$. This contradicts the assumption that $a$ and $b$ are coprime.
