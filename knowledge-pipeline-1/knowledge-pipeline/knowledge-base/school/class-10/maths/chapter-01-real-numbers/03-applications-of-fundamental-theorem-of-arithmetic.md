The Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic is used to find the HCF and LCM of integers.

**Example: Find the LCM and HCF of 6 and 20.**
Solution: 
$6 = 2^1 \times 3^1$
$20 = 2^2 \times 5^1$

*   $HCF(6, 20) = 2^1 = 2$ (Product of the smallest power of each common prime factor).
*   $LCM(6, 20) = 2^2 \times 3^1 \times 5^1 = 60$ (Product of the greatest power of each prime factor involved).

For any two positive integers $a$ and $b$:
$HCF(a, b) \times LCM(a, b) = a \times b$

**Example: Find HCF and LCM of 6, 72, and 120.**
$6 = 2 \times 3$
$72 = 2^3 \times 3^2$
$120 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 5$

*   $HCF(6, 72, 120) = 2^1 \times 3^1 = 6$
*   $LCM(6, 72, 120) = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5^1 = 360$

*Note: The product of three numbers is not equal to the product of their HCF and LCM.*
