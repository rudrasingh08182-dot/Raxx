Key points:
1. Every composite number has a unique prime factorisation.
2. If $p$ is prime and $p|a^2$, then $p|a$.
3. $\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}$ are irrational.

**Note for three numbers $p, q, r$:**
$LCM(p, q, r) = \frac{p \cdot q \cdot r \cdot HCF(p, q, r)}{HCF(p, q) \cdot HCF(q, r) \cdot HCF(p, r)}$
$HCF(p, q, r) = \frac{p \cdot q \cdot r \cdot LCM(p, q, r)}{LCM(p, q) \cdot LCM(q, r) \cdot LCM(p, r)}$
