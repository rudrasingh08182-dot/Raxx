1. Express 140, 156, 3825, 5005, and 7429 as products of prime factors.
2. Find the LCM and HCF of (26, 91), (510, 92), and (336, 54) and verify $LCM \times HCF = \text{product of numbers}$.
3. Find the LCM and HCF of (12, 15, 21), (17, 23, 29), and (8, 9, 25) using prime factorisation.
4. Given $HCF(306, 657) = 9$, find $LCM(306, 657)$.
5. Check if $6^n$ can end with the digit 0 for any natural number $n$.
6. Prove that $\sqrt{5}$, $3+2\sqrt{5}$, $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$, $7\sqrt{5}$, and $6+\sqrt{2}$ are irrational.
