Key takeaways:
- Graphical method: Intersecting (unique), Parallel (none), Coincident (infinite).
- Algebraic methods: Substitution and Elimination.
- Consistency conditions:
  - $\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$: Consistent.
  - $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \frac{c_1}{c_2}$: Inconsistent.
  - $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2}$: Dependent and Consistent.
