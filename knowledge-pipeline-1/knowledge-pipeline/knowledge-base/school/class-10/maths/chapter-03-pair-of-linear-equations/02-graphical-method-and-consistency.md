A pair of linear equations can be analyzed by their graphical representation. The relationship between the coefficients of the equations $a_1x + b_1y + c_1 = 0$ and $a_2x + b_2y + c_2 = 0$ determines the nature of the solution:

| Ratio Comparison | Graphical Representation | Algebraic Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\frac{a_1}{a_2} \neq \frac{b_1}{b_2}$ | Intersecting lines | Exactly one solution (Unique) |
| $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} = \frac{c_1}{c_2}$ | Coincident lines | Infinitely many solutions |
| $\frac{a_1}{a_2} = \frac{b_1}{b_2} \neq \frac{c_1}{c_2}$ | Parallel lines | No solution |

- **Consistent pair:** Has at least one solution (intersecting or coincident).
- **Inconsistent pair:** Has no solution (parallel).
- **Dependent pair:** A consistent pair with infinitely many solutions (coincident).
