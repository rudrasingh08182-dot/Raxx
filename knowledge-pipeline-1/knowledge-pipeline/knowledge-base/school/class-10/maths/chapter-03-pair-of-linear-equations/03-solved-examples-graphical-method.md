Example: Check if $x + 3y = 6$ and $2x - 3y = 12$ is consistent.

1. Find two points for each line:
   - For $x + 3y = 6$: $(0, 2)$ and $(6, 0)$.
   - For $2x - 3y = 12$: $(0, -4)$ and $(3, -2)$.
2. Plotting these points reveals that the lines intersect at $(6, 0)$.
3. Since they intersect, the system is consistent and the solution is $x=6, y=0$.
