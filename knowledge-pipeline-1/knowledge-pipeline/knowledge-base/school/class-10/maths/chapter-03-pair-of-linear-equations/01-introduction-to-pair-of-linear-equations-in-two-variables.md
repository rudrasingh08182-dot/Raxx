A pair of linear equations in two variables can be used to represent real-life situations. For example, if Akhila plays Hoopla ($y$ times) and rides a Giant Wheel ($x$ times), the situation can be modeled as:
$y = \frac{1}{2}x$
$3x + 4y = 20$

These equations represent relationships between two unknown quantities. Solving such pairs of equations allows us to find the specific values of $x$ and $y$ that satisfy both conditions simultaneously.
