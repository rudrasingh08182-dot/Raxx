A quadratic equation in variable $x$ is defined as $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, where $a, b, c$ are real numbers and $a \neq 0$. This is known as the standard form. Any equation $p(x) = 0$, where $p(x)$ is a polynomial of degree 2, can be rearranged into this standard form by writing terms in descending order of their degrees.

Example: Representing situations mathematically:
1. If John and Jivanti have 45 marbles total and lose 5 each, with a product of 124 remaining, the equation is $x^2 - 45x + 324 = 0$.
2. If a cottage industry produces $x$ toys at a cost of $(55 - x)$ each, with a total cost of 750, the equation is $x^2 - 55x + 750 = 0$.
