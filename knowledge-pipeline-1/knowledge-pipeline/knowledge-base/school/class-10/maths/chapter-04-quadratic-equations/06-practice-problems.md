1. Find the roots of $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$ by factorisation.
2. Find the values of $k$ for $2x^2 + kx + 3 = 0$ so that it has two equal roots.
3. The altitude of a right triangle is 7 cm less than its base. If the hypotenuse is 13 cm, find the other two sides.
4. Is it possible to design a rectangular park of perimeter 80 m and area 400 m$^2$? If so, find its length and breadth.
