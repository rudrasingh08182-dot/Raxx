**Example 1:** Find the area of the sector of a circle with radius 4 cm and angle $30^\circ$ (Use $\pi = 3.14$).
*Solution:* 
$\text{Area} = \frac{30}{360} \times 3.14 \times 4 \times 4 = \frac{1}{12} \times 50.24 \approx 4.19 \text{ cm}^2$.
$\text{Area of major sector} = \pi r^2 - 4.19 = (3.14 \times 16) - 4.19 = 50.24 - 4.19 = 46.05 \text{ cm}^2$.

**Example 2:** Find the area of the segment AYB if radius is 21 cm and $\angle AOB = 120^\circ$ (Use $\pi = 22/7$).
*Solution:* 
$\text{Area of sector} = \frac{120}{360} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 21 \times 21 = 462 \text{ cm}^2$.
$\text{Area of } \triangle OAB = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{441}{4}\sqrt{3} \text{ cm}^2$.
$\text{Area of segment} = 462 - \frac{441}{4}\sqrt{3} \text{ cm}^2$.
