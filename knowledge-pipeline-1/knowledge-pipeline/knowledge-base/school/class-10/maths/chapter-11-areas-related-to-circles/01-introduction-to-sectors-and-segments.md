A sector of a circle is the portion of the circular region enclosed by two radii and the corresponding arc. The angle formed between the two radii at the centre is called the angle of the sector. A circle can have a minor sector and a major sector, where the angle of the major sector is $360^\circ - \angle AOB$.

A segment of a circle is the portion of the circular region enclosed between a chord and the corresponding arc. Similar to sectors, segments are divided into minor segments and major segments. By convention, the terms 'sector' and 'segment' refer to the minor sector and minor segment, respectively, unless otherwise specified.
