Key points covered in this chapter:
1. Length of an arc of a sector: $\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi r$.
2. Area of a sector: $\frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2$.
3. Area of a segment: $\text{Area of sector} - \text{Area of triangle}$.
