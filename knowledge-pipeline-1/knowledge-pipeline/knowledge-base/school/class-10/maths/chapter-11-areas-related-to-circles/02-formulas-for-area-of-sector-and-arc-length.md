Using the unitary method, we can derive the formulas for the area of a sector and the length of an arc based on the angle $\theta$ subtended at the centre:

1. **Area of a sector of angle $\theta$:**
   $$\text{Area} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2$$
   where $r$ is the radius of the circle.

2. **Length of an arc of a sector of angle $\theta$:**
   $$\text{Length} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi r$$

**Note:** The area of a major sector can be calculated as $\pi r^2 - \text{Area of the minor sector}$ or by using the formula $\frac{360^\circ - \theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2$.
