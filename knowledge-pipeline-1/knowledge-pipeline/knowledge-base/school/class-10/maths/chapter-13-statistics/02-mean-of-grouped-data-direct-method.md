The mean (or average) of observations is the sum of the values of all observations divided by the total number of observations. For grouped data, if $x_i$ are observations with frequencies $f_i$, the mean $\bar{x}$ is given by:

$$\bar{x} = \frac{\sum f_i x_i}{\sum f_i}$$

This is known as the Direct Method. When data is large, we often condense it into class intervals and use the class mark (mid-point) as the representative $x_i$ for each class:

$$\text{Class mark} = \frac{\text{Upper class limit} + \text{Lower class limit}}{2}$$
