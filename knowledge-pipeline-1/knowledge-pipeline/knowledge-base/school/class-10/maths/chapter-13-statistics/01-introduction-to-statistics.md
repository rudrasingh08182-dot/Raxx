In Class IX, you studied the classification of data into ungrouped and grouped frequency distributions, along with their pictorial representations like bar graphs, histograms, and frequency polygons. You also learned about measures of central tendency: mean, median, and mode for ungrouped data. In this chapter, we extend these concepts to grouped data and introduce cumulative frequency and ogives.
