The choice of central tendency depends on the data:
- **Mean:** Best when all observations are important and the data is not heavily skewed by extreme values.
- **Median:** Best when individual observations are less important or when extreme values exist (e.g., average wage).
- **Mode:** Best for identifying the most popular or frequent item.

**Empirical Relationship:** $3 \text{ Median} = \text{Mode} + 2 \text{ Mean}$
