The coordinates of the point $P(x, y)$ which divides the line segment joining the points $A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2, y_2)$ internally in the ratio $m_1 : m_2$ are:

$x = \frac{m_1x_2 + m_2x_1}{m_1 + m_2}, \quad y = \frac{m_1y_2 + m_2y_1}{m_1 + m_2}$

**Special Case (Mid-point):**
The mid-point of a line segment divides it in the ratio $1 : 1$. The coordinates are:
$(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2})$
