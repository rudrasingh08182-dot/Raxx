1. Find the distance between $(2, 3)$ and $(4, 1)$.
2. Determine if the points $(1, 5)$, $(2, 3)$ and $(-2, -11)$ are collinear.
3. Find the coordinates of the point which divides the join of $(-1, 7)$ and $(4, -3)$ in the ratio $2 : 3$.
4. Find the ratio in which the line segment joining $A(1, -5)$ and $B(-4, 5)$ is divided by the x-axis.
5. Find the area of a rhombus if its vertices are $(3, 0)$, $(4, 5)$, $(-1, 4)$ and $(-2, -1)$ taken in order.
