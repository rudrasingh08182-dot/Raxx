1. Distance between $P(x_1, y_1)$ and $Q(x_2, y_2)$ is $\sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$.
2. Distance of $P(x, y)$ from origin is $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$.
3. Section formula for ratio $m_1 : m_2$: $(\frac{m_1x_2 + m_2x_1}{m_1 + m_2}, \frac{m_1y_2 + m_2y_1}{m_1 + m_2})$.
4. Mid-point formula: $(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2})$.
