The $n$th term ($a_n$) of an AP with first term $a$ and common difference $d$ is given by the formula:
$a_n = a + (n - 1)d$

Here, $a_n$ is also called the general term of the AP. If there are $m$ terms in the AP, the last term is often denoted by $l$.
