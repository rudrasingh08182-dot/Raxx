The sum ($S_n$) of the first $n$ terms of an AP is given by:
$S_n = \frac{n}{2} [2a + (n - 1)d]$

Alternatively, if the first term $a$ and the last term $l$ are known:
$S_n = \frac{n}{2} (a + l)$

Note: The $n$th term can also be found using the sum formula: $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1}$.
