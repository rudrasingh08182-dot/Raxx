In nature and daily life, we observe many patterns. For example, the petals of a sunflower, the grains on a maize cob, or financial scenarios like annual salary increments or savings schemes. In this chapter, we focus on a specific type of pattern where succeeding terms are obtained by adding a fixed number to the preceding terms. This pattern is known as an Arithmetic Progression (AP).
