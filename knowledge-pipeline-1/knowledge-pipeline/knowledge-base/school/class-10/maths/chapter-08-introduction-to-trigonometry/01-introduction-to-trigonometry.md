Trigonometry is a branch of mathematics derived from the Greek words 'tri' (three), 'gon' (sides), and 'metron' (measure). It is the study of relationships between the sides and angles of a triangle. Historically, it was used by early astronomers in Egypt and Babylon to calculate distances of stars and planets. Today, it is fundamental to engineering and physical sciences. In this chapter, we focus on trigonometric ratios of acute angles in right-angled triangles.
