Key takeaways from the chapter:
- Similar figures have the same shape but not necessarily the same size.
- Two polygons are similar if corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional.
- Basic Proportionality Theorem relates parallel lines in a triangle to proportional segments.
- Similarity criteria for triangles include AAA, AA, SSS, and SAS.
- An additional RHS Similarity Criterion exists for right triangles: if the hypotenuse and one side of one triangle are proportional to the hypotenuse and one side of another, the triangles are similar.
