Example: In $\triangle ABC$, if $DE \parallel BC$, prove that $\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{AE}{AC}$.

Solution:
Since $DE \parallel BC$, by Theorem 6.1:
$$\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$$
Taking the reciprocal:
$$\frac{DB}{AD} = \frac{EC}{AE}$$
Adding 1 to both sides:
$$\frac{DB}{AD} + 1 = \frac{EC}{AE} + 1 \implies \frac{DB+AD}{AD} = \frac{EC+AE}{AE} \implies \frac{AB}{AD} = \frac{AC}{AE}$$
Therefore, $\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{AE}{AC}$.
