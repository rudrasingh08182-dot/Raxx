### ANIMAL KINGDOM

Dry and cornified skin is a characteristic feature of reptiles. Limbs are absent in snakes. Fishes, amphibians, and reptiles are poikilothermous (cold-blooded). Aves are warm-blooded animals with feathers on their bodies and forelimbs modified into wings for flying. Hind limbs are adapted for walking, swimming, perching, or clasping. The unique features of mammals are the presence of mammary glands and hairs on the skin. They commonly exhibit viviparity.
