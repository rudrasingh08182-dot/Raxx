In terms of shape and size also, considerable degree of variability is observed. Typically it is sausage-shaped or cylindrical having a diameter of 0.2-1.0 µm (average 0.5 µm) and length 1.0-4.1 µm. Each mitochondrion is a double membrane-bound structure.
