This membrane is similar structurally to that of the eukaryotes. A special membranous structure is the mesosome which is formed by the extensions of plasma membrane into the cell. These extensions are in the form of vesicles, tubules and lamellae. They help in cell wall formation, DNA replication and respiration.

| Organism | Size |
| :--- | :--- |
| Typical bacteria | 1-2 $\mu m$ |
| PPLO | about 0.1 $\mu m$ |
| Viruses | 0.02-0.2 $\mu m$ |
| A typical eukaryotic cell | 10-20 $\mu m$ |
