12.7 RESPIRATORY QUOTIENT

Let us now look at another aspect of respiration. As you know, during aerobic respiration, $O_2$ is consumed and $CO_2$ is released. The ratio of the volume of $CO_2$ evolved to the volume of $O_2$ consumed in respiration is called the respiratory quotient (RQ) or respiratory ratio.

$RQ = \frac{\text{volume of } CO_2 \text{ evolved}}{\text{volume of } O_2 \text{ consumed}}$

The respiratory quotient depends upon the type of respiratory substrate used during respiration.
