### BIOMOLECULES 107

*   **Cholesterol**
*   **Phospholipid (Lecithin)**
*   **Fats and oils (lipids)**
*   **Fatty acid (Palmitic acid):** $(CH_2)_{14}CH_3COOH$
*   **Glycerol:** Triglyceride ($R_1, R_2$ and $R_3$ are fatty acids)
*   **Nitrogen bases:** Adenine, Uracil
*   **Nucleotide:** Adenylic acid
*   **Nucleosides:** Adenosine, Uridine
*   **Sugars (Carbohydrates):** $C_6H_{12}O_6$ (Glucose), $C_5H_{10}O_5$ (Ribose)
*   **Amino acids:** Serine, Glycine, Alanine
