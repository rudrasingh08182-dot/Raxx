These ionic gradients across the resting membrane are maintained by the active transport of ions by the sodium-potassium pump which transports 3 $Na^+$ outwards for 2 $K^+$ into the cell. As a result, the outer surface of the axonal membrane possesses a positive charge while its inner surface possesses a negative charge.
