### 6.4 Criteria for Similarity of Triangles

In the previous section, we stated that two triangles are similar, if (i) their corresponding angles are equal and (ii) their corresponding sides are in the same ratio (or proportion). That is, in $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta DEF$, if
(i) $\angle A = \angle D, \angle B = \angle E, \angle C = \angle F$ and
(ii) $\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{BC}{EF} = \frac{CA}{FD}$, then the two triangles are similar (see Fig. 6.22).
