A NOTE TO THE READER
You have seen that:
$HCF(p, q, r) \times LCM(p, q, r) \neq p \times q \times r$, where $p, q, r$ are positive integers (see Example 8). However, the following results hold good for three numbers $p, q$ and $r$:

$LCM(p, q, r) = \frac{p \cdot q \cdot r \cdot HCF(p, q, r)}{HCF(p, q) \cdot HCF(q, r) \cdot HCF(p, r)}$

$HCF(p, q, r) = \frac{p \cdot q \cdot r \cdot LCM(p, q, r)}{LCM(p, q) \cdot LCM(q, r) \cdot LCM(p, r)}$
