The absolute minimum temperature for an ideal gas, therefore, is inferred by extrapolating the straight line to the axis, as in Fig. 10.3. This temperature is found to be – 273.15 °C and is designated as absolute zero. Absolute zero is the foundation of the Kelvin temperature scale or absolute scale temperature.
