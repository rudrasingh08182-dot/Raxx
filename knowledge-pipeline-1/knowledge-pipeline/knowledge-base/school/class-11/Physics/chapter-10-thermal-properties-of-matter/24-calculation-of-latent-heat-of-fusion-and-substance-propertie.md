**Answer**

Heat lost by water = $m_s w (\theta_f - \theta_i)_w$
= (0.30 kg) (4186 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹) (50.0 °C – 6.7 °C)
= 54376.14 J

Heat required to melt ice = $m_2 L_f = (0.15 kg) L_f$

Heat required to raise temperature of ice water to final temperature = $m_I s_w (\theta_f - \theta_i)_I$
= (0.15 kg) (4186 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹) (6.7 °C – 0 °C)
= 4206.93 J

Heat lost = heat gained
54376.14 J = (0.15 kg) $L_f$ + 4206.93 J
$L_f = 3.34 \times 10^5$ J kg⁻¹.
