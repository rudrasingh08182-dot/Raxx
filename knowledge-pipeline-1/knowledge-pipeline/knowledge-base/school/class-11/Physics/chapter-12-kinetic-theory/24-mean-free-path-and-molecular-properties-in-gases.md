So, $25 \times 25 \times 10^{-3} / 273.2 \times 10^2 = 3.73 \times 10^{-4} \text{ m}$.

Hence, mean free path $l = 4 \times 10^{-7} \text{ m}$.

Note that the mean free path is 100 times the interatomic distance $\approx 40 \text{ Å} = 4 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$ calculated earlier. It is this large value of mean free path that leads to the typical gaseous behaviour. Gases cannot be confined without a container. Using the kinetic theory of gases, the bulk measurable properties like viscosity, heat conductivity and diffusion can be related to the microscopic parameters like molecular size. It is through such relations that the molecular sizes were first estimated.
