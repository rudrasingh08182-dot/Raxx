In three dimensions, the average energy is $3 k_B T$. For a mole of solid, $N = N_A$, and the total energy is $U = 3 k_B T \times N_A = 3 RT$. Now at constant pressure $\Delta Q = \Delta U + P\Delta V = \Delta U$, since for a solid $\Delta V$ is negligible. Hence, $\Delta Q = \Delta U = 3 R \Delta T$ (12.37).
