We next consider some examples which give us information about the volume occupied by the molecules and the volume of a single molecule. Example 12.1: The density of water is $1000 \text{ kg m}^{-3}$. The density of water vapour at $100 \text{ °C}$ and $1 \text{ atm}$ pressure is $0.6 \text{ kg m}^{-3}$. The volume of a molecule multiplied by the total number gives, what is called, molecular volume. Estimate the ratio (or fraction) of the molecular volume to the total volume occupied by the water vapour under the above conditions of temperature and pressure.
