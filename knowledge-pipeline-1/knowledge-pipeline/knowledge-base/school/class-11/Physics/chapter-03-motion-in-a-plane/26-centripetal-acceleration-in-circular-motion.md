The acceleration is directed, at each point of the path, towards the centre of the circle. In the limit $\Delta t \to 0$, $\Delta r$ becomes perpendicular to $r$. In this limit $\Delta v \to 0$ and is consequently also perpendicular to $v$. Therefore, the acceleration is directed towards the centre, at each point of the circular path.
