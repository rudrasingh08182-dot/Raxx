MOTION IN A PLANE 45

The maximum height $H$ reached by a projectile is given by:
$H = \frac{v_0^2 \sin^2 \theta}{2g}$

The time taken to reach this height is:
$t_m = \frac{v_0 \sin \theta}{g}$

The horizontal distance travelled by a projectile from its initial position to the position it passes $y = 0$ during its fall is called the range, $R$ of the projectile. It is:
$R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$

17. When an object follows a circular path at constant speed, the motion of the object is called uniform circular motion. The magnitude of its acceleration is $a_c = \frac{v^2}{R}$. The direction of $a_c$ is always towards the centre of the circle.

The angular speed $\omega$, is the rate of change of angular distance. It is related to velocity $v$ by $v = \omega R$. The acceleration is $a_c = \omega^2 R$.

If $T$ is the time period of revolution of the object in circular motion and $\nu$ is its frequency, we have $\omega = 2\pi \nu$, $v = 2\pi \nu R$, $a_c = 4\pi^2 \nu^2 R$.
