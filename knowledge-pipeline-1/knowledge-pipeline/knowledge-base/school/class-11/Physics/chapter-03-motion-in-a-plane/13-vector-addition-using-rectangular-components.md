It is much easier to add vectors by combining their respective components. Consider two vectors $\vec{A}$ and $\vec{B}$ in the x-y plane with components $A_x, A_y$ and $B_x, B_y$:

$\vec{A} = A_x \hat{i} + A_y \hat{j}$ (3.18)
