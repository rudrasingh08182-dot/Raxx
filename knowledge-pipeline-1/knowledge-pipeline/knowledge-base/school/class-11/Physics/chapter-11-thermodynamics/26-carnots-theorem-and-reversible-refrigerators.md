This will amount to taking heat $Q_2$ from the cold reservoir at $T_2$, doing work $W$ on the system, and transferring heat $Q_1$ to the hot reservoir. This will be a reversible refrigerator. We next establish the important result (sometimes called Carnot’s theorem) that (a) working between two given temperatures $T_1$ and $T_2$ of the hot and cold reservoirs respectively, no engine can have efficiency more than that of the Carnot engine and (b) the efficiency of the Carnot engine is independent of the nature of the working substance.
