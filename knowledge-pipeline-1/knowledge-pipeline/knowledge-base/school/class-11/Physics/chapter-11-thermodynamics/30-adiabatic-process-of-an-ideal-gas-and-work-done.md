In an adiabatic process of an ideal gas $PV^{\gamma} = \text{constant}$, where $\gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v}$.

Work done by an ideal gas in an adiabatic change of state from $(P_1, V_1, T_1)$ to $(P_2, V_2, T_2)$ is:

$W = \frac{\mu R(T_1 - T_2)}{\gamma - 1}$
