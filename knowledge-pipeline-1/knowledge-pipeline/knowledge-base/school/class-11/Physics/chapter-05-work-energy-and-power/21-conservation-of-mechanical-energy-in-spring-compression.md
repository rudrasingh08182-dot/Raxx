$\frac{1}{2} k x_m^2 = K = 1.25 \times 10^4 \text{ J}$

Where we have converted $18 \text{ km h}^{-1}$ to $5 \text{ m s}^{-1}$ [It is useful to remember that $36 \text{ km h}^{-1} = 10 \text{ m s}^{-1}$].

At maximum compression $x_m$, the potential energy $V$ of the spring is equal to the kinetic energy $K$ of the moving car from the principle of conservation of mechanical energy.

$\frac{1}{2} k x_m^2 = V$
