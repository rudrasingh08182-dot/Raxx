$\frac{1}{2} m v^2 - \frac{1}{2} k x_m^2 = 0$

where we take the positive square root since $x_m$ is positive. Putting in numerical values we obtain

$x_m = 1.35 \text{ m}$

which, as expected, is less than the result in Example 5.8. If the two forces on the body consist of a conservative force $F_c$ and a non-conservative force $F_{nc}$, the conservation of mechanical energy formula will have to be modified. By the WE theorem

$(F_c + F_{nc}) \Delta x = \Delta K$

But $F_c \Delta x = - \Delta V$

Hence, $\Delta(K + V) = F_{nc} \Delta x$

$\Delta E = F_{nc} \Delta x$

where $E$ is the total mechanical energy. Over the path this assumes the form

$E_f - E_i = W_{nc}$

where $W_{nc}$ is the total work done by the non-conservative forces over the path.
