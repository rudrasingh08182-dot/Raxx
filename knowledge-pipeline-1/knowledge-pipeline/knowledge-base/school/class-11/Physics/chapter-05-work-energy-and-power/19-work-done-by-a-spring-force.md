WORK, ENERGY AND POWER 81

Fig. 5.8 Parabolic plots of the potential energy V and kinetic energy K of a block attached to a spring obeying Hooke’s law. The two plots are complementary, one decreasing as the other increases. The total mechanical energy $E = K + V$ remains constant.

The external force $F$ does work $+kx^2/2$. If the block is moved from an initial displacement $x_i$ to a final displacement $x_f$, the work done by the spring force $W_s$ is:

$W_s = -\int_{x_i}^{x_f} kx dx = -\left[\frac{1}{2}kx_f^2 - \frac{1}{2}kx_i^2\right] = \frac{1}{2}kx_i^2 - \frac{1}{2}kx_f^2$ (5.17)
