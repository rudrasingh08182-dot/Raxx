**Example 7.5**

The planet Mars has two moons, Phobos and Deimos. (i) Phobos has a period of 7 hours, 39 minutes and an orbital radius of $9.4 \times 10^3 \text{ km}$. Calculate the mass of Mars. (ii) Assume that Earth and Mars move in circular orbits around the Sun, with the Martian orbit being 1.52 times the orbital radius of the Earth. What is the length of the Martian year in days?

**Answer**

(i) We employ Eq. (7.38) with the Sun’s mass replaced by the Martian mass $M_m$:

$T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2 R^3}{GM_m}$

$M_m = \frac{4\pi^2 R^3}{GT^2}$

$M_m = \frac{4 \times (3.14)^2 \times (9.4 \times 10^6)^3}{6.67 \times 10^{-11} \times (27540)^2}$

$M_m = 6.48 \times 10^{23} \text{ kg}$.

(ii) Once again Kepler’s third law comes to our aid,

$\frac{T_M^2}{T_E^2} = \frac{R_{MS}^3}{R_{ES}^3}$
