Fig. 7.8 (b) g at a depth d. In this case only the smaller sphere of radius $(R_E - d)$ contributes to g. Thus the force on the point mass is
$F(d) = \frac{G M_s m}{(R_E - d)^2}$ (7.17)
Substituting for $M_s$ from above, we get
$F(d) = \frac{G M_E m (R_E - d)}{R_E^3}$ (7.18)
and hence the acceleration due to gravity at a depth d,
$g(d) = \frac{F(d)}{m} = \frac{G M_E (R_E - d)}{R_E^3} = g \left(1 - \frac{d}{R_E}\right)$ (7.19)
Thus, as we go down below earth's surface, the acceleration due gravity decreases by a factor $(1 - d/R_E)$.
