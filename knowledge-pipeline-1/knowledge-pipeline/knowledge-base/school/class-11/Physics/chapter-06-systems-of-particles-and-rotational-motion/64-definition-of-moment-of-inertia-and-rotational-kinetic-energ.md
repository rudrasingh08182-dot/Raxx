The moment of inertia of a rigid body about an axis is defined by the formula $I = \sum m_i r_i^2$, where $r_i$ is the perpendicular distance of the $i^{th}$ point of the body from the axis. The kinetic energy of rotation is $K = \frac{1}{2} I \omega^2$.
