6.17 The oxygen molecule has a mass of $5.30 \times 10^{-26} \text{ kg}$ and a moment of inertia of $1.94 \times 10^{-46} \text{ kg m}^2$ about an axis through its centre perpendicular to the line joining the two atoms. Suppose the mean speed of such a molecule in a gas is $500 \text{ m/s}$ and that its kinetic energy of rotation is two-thirds of its kinetic energy of translation. Find the average angular velocity of the molecule.
