Forces which are parallel to the axis will give torques perpendicular to the axis and need not be taken into account. (2) We need to consider only those components of the position vectors which are perpendicular to the axis. Components of position vectors along the axis will result in torques perpendicular to the axis and need not be taken into account. Work done by a torque.
