Here $K_1$, $K_2$ and $K_3$ are constants; $L_x$, $L_y$ and $L_z$ are the components of the total angular momentum vector $L$ along the x, y and z axes respectively. The statement that the total angular momentum is conserved means that each of these three components is conserved. Eq. (6.29a) is the rotational analogue of Eq. (6.18a), i.e., the conservation law of the total linear momentum for a system of particles. Like Eq. (6.18a), it has applications in many practical situations.
