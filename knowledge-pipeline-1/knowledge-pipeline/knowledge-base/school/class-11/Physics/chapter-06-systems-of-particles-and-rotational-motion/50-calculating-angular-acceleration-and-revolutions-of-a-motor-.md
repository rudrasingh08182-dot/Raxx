This derivation and the derivation of Eq. (6.38) is left as an exercise. Example 6.11: The angular speed of a motor wheel is increased from 1200 rpm to 3120 rpm in 16 seconds. (i) What is its angular acceleration, assuming the acceleration to be uniform? (ii) How many revolutions does the engine make during this time? Answer: (i) We shall use $\omega = \omega_0 + \alpha t$, where $\omega_0$ is the initial angular speed in rad/s.
