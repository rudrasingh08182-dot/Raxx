The angular momentum $L$ and the angular velocity $\omega$ are not necessarily parallel vectors. However, for the simpler situations discussed in this chapter when rotation is about a fixed axis which is an axis of symmetry of the rigid body, the relation $L = I\omega$ holds good, where $I$ is the moment of inertia of the body about the rotation axis.
