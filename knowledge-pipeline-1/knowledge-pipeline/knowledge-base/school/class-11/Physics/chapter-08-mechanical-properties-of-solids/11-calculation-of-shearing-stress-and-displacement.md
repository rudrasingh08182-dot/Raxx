The area of the face parallel to which this force is applied is $A = 50 \text{ cm} \times 10 \text{ cm} = 0.5 \text{ m} \times 0.1 \text{ m} = 0.05 \text{ m}^2$. Therefore, the stress applied is $= (9.4 \times 10^4 \text{ N} / 0.05 \text{ m}^2) = 1.80 \times 10^6 \text{ N m}^{-2}$.

Fig. 8.5

We know that shearing strain $= (\Delta x / L) = \text{Stress} / G$. Therefore the displacement $\Delta x = (\text{Stress} \times L) / G = (1.8 \times 10^6 \text{ N m}^{-2} \times 0.5 \text{ m}) / (5.6 \times 10^9 \text{ N m}^{-2}) = 1.6 \times 10^{-4} \text{ m} = 0.16 \text{ mm}$.
