The moment after a stone is released out of an accelerated train, there is no horizontal force (or acceleration) on the stone, if the effects of the surrounding air are neglected. The stone then has only the vertical force of gravity. In the second law of motion $F = ma$, $F$ stands for the net force due to all material agencies external to the body. $a$ is the effect of the force. $ma$ should not be regarded as yet another force, besides $F$.
