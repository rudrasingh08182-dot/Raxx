(b) The system (block + cylinder) accelerates downwards with $0.1 \text{ m s}^{-2}$. The free-body diagram of the system shows two forces on the system: the force of gravity due to the earth (270 N); and the normal force $R'$ by the floor. Note, the free-body diagram of the system does not show the internal forces between the block and the cylinder. Applying the second law to the system,

$270 - R' = 27 \times 0.1 \text{ N}$

i.e., $R' = 267.3 \text{ N}$

Fig. 4.15

By the third law, the action of the system on the floor is equal to 267.3 N vertically downward. Action-reaction pairs:

For (a): (i) the force of gravity (20 N) on the block by the earth (say, action); the force of gravity on the earth by the block (reaction) equal to 20 N directed upwards (not shown in the figure). (ii) the force on the floor by the block (action); the force on the block by the floor (reaction).

For (b): (i) the force of gravity (270 N) on the system by the earth (say, action); the force of gravity on the earth by the system (reaction), equal to 270 N.
