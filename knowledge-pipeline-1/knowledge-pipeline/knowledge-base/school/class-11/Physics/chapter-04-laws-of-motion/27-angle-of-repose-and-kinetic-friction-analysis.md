Resolving the weight $mg$ along the two directions shown, we have:
$mg \sin \theta = f_s$, $mg \cos \theta = N$

As $\theta$ increases, the self-adjusting frictional force $f_s$ increases until at $\theta = \theta_{max}$, $f_s$ achieves its maximum value, $(f_s)_{max} = \mu_s N$. Therefore,
$\tan \theta_{max} = \mu_s$ or $\theta_{max} = \tan^{-1} \mu_s$

When $\theta$ becomes just a little more than $\theta_{max}$, there is a small net force on the block and it begins to slide. Note that $\theta_{max}$ depends only on $\mu_s$ and is independent of the mass of the block. For $\theta_{max} = 15^\circ$,
$\mu_s = \tan 15^\circ = 0.27$
