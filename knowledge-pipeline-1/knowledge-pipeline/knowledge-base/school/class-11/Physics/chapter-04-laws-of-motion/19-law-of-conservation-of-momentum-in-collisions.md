The total momentum of an isolated system of interacting particles is conserved. An important example of the application of the law of conservation of momentum is the collision of two bodies. Consider two bodies A and B, with initial momenta $p_A$ and $p_B$. The bodies collide, get apart, with final momenta $p'_A$ and $p'_B$ respectively. By the Second Law $F_{AB} \Delta t = p'_A - p_A$.
