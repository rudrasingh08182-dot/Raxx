$S_{la} \cos \theta + S_{sl} = S_{sa}$ (9.22)

The angle of contact is an obtuse angle if $S_{sl} > S_{la}$ as in the case of water-leaf interface, while it is an acute angle if $S_{sl} < S_{la}$ as in the case of water-plastic interface. When $\theta$ is an obtuse angle then molecules of liquids are attracted strongly to themselves and weakly to those of solid, it costs a lot of energy to create a liquid-solid surface, and liquid then does not wet the solid. This is what happens with water on a waxy or oily surface, and with mercury on glass.
