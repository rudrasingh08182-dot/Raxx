Note the dramatic effect in the graph (c); the displacements due to the two pulses have exactly cancelled each other and there is zero displacement throughout. To put the principle of superposition mathematically, let $y_1(x,t)$ and $y_2(x,t)$ be the displacements due to two wave disturbances in the medium. If the waves arrive in a region simultaneously, and therefore, overlap, the net displacement $y(x,t)$ is given by:

$y(x, t) = y_1(x, t) + y_2(x, t)$ (14.25)
