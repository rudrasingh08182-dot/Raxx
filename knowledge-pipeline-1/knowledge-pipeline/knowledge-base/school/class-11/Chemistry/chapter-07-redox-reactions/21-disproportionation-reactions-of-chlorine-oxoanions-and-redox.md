**Problem 7.5**
Which of the following species do not show disproportionation reaction and why? $ClO^-$, $ClO_2^-$, $ClO_3^-$ and $ClO_4^-$. Also write reaction for each of the species that disproportionates.

**Solution**
Among the oxoanions of chlorine listed above, $ClO_4^-$ does not disproportionate because in this oxoanion chlorine is present in its highest oxidation state that is, +7. The disproportionation reactions for the other three oxoanions of chlorine are as follows:

$3ClO^- \rightarrow 2Cl^- + ClO_3^-$

$6ClO_2^- \rightarrow 4ClO_3^- + 2Cl^-$

$4ClO_3^- \rightarrow Cl^- + 3ClO_4^-$
