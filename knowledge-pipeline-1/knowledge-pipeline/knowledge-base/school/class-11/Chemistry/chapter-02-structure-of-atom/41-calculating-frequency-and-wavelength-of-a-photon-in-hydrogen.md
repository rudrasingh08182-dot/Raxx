### Problem 2.10
What are the frequency and wavelength of a photon emitted during a transition from $n = 5$ state to the $n = 2$ state in the hydrogen atom?

### Solution
Since $n_i = 5$ and $n_f = 2$, this transition gives rise to a spectral line in the visible region of the Balmer series. From equation (2.17):

$\Delta E = 2.18 \times 10^{-18} \text{ J} \left( \frac{1}{n_i^2} - \frac{1}{n_f^2} \right)$
