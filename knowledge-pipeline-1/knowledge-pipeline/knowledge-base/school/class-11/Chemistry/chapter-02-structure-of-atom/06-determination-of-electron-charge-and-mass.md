He found the charge on the electron to be $-1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ C. The present accepted value of electrical charge is $-1.602176 \times 10^{-19}$ C. The mass of the electron ($m_e$) was determined by combining these results with Thomson’s value of $e/m_e$ ratio. $m_e = 9.1094 \times 10^{-31}$ kg (2.2)
