EQUILIBRIUM

Calculate $K_c$ for the reaction,
$PCl_5 \rightleftharpoons PCl_3 + Cl_2$

Solution
The equilibrium constant $K_c$ for the above reaction can be written as,
$K_c = \frac{[PCl_3][Cl_2]}{[PCl_5]}$
