6.59 The ionization constant of propanoic acid is $1.32 \times 10^{-5}$. Calculate the degree of ionization of the acid in its $0.05\text{M}$ solution and also its pH. What will be its degree of ionization if the solution is $0.01\text{M}$ in HCl also?

6.60 The pH of $0.1\text{M}$ solution of cyanic acid (HCNO) is $2.34$. Calculate the ionization constant of the acid and its degree of ionization in the solution.

6.61 The ionization constant of nitrous acid is $4.5 \times 10^{-4}$. Calculate the pH of $0.04\text{M}$ sodium nitrite solution and also its degree of hydrolysis.

6.62 A $0.02\text{M}$ solution of pyridinium hydrochloride has $\text{pH} = 3.44$. Calculate the ionization constant of pyridine.

6.63 Predict if the solutions of the following salts are neutral, acidic or basic:
NaCl, KBr, NaCN, $\text{NH}_4\text{NO}_3$, $\text{NaNO}_2$ and KF

6.64 The ionization constant of chloroacetic acid is $1.35 \times 10^{-3}$. What will be the pH of $0.1\text{M}$ acid and its $0.1\text{M}$ sodium salt solution?

6.65 Ionic product of water at $310\text{K}$ is $2.7 \times 10^{-14}$. What is the pH of neutral water at this temperature?

6.66 Calculate the pH of the resultant mixtures:
a) $10\text{ mL}$ of $0.2\text{M } \text{Ca(OH)}_2 + 25\text{ mL}$ of $0.1\text{M HCl}$
b) $10\text{ mL}$ of $0.01\text{M H}_2\text{SO}_4 + 10\text{ mL}$ of $0.01\text{M Ca(OH)}_2$
c) $10\text{ mL}$ of $0.1\text{M H}_2\text{SO}_4 + 10\text{ mL}$ of $0.1\text{M KOH}$

6.67 Determine the solubilities of silver chromate, barium chromate, ferric hydroxide, lead chloride and mercurous iodide at $298\text{K}$ from their solubility product constants given in Table 6.9. Determine also the molarities of individual ions.

6.68 The solubility product constant of $\text{Ag}_2\text{CrO}_4$ and $\text{AgBr}$ are $1.1 \times 10^{-12}$ and $5.0 \times 10^{-13}$ respectively. Calculate the ratio of the molarities of their saturated solutions.

6.69 Equal volumes of $0.002\text{M}$ solutions of sodium iodate and cupric chlorate are mixed together. Will it lead to precipitation of copper iodate? (For cupric iodate $K_{sp} = 7.4 \times 10^{-8}$).

6.70 The ionization constant of benzoic acid is $6.46 \times 10^{-5}$ and $K_{sp}$ for silver benzoate is $2.5 \times 10^{-13}$. How many times is silver benzoate more soluble in a buffer of pH $3.19$ compared to its solubility in pure water?
