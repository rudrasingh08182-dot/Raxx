$\frac{[X^-]}{[HX]} = \frac{K_a}{[H^+]}$

Taking inverse of both sides and adding 1, we get:

$\frac{[HX]}{[X^-]} + 1 = \frac{[H^+]}{K_a} + 1$

$\frac{[HX] + [X^-]}{[X^-]} = \frac{[H^+] + K_a}{K_a}$

Now, again taking inverse, we get:

$\frac{[X^-]}{[X^-] + [HX]} = f = \frac{K_a}{K_a + [H^+]}$

It can be seen that 'f' decreases as pH decreases.

If S is the solubility of the salt at a given pH, then:

$K_{sp} = [S] [f S] = S^2 \left\{ \frac{K_a}{K_a + [H^+]} \right\}$

And

$S = \left\{ \frac{K_{sp} ([H^+] + K_a)}{K_a} \right\}^{1/2} \quad (6.46)$

Thus, solubility S increases with increase in $[H^+]$ or decrease in pH.
