This $NH_3$ exists in the following equilibrium:

$NH_4OH \rightleftharpoons NH_4^+ + OH^-$

$0.033M - y \quad \quad \quad y \quad \quad y$

where, $y = [OH^-] = [NH_4^+]$

The final 75 mL solution after neutralisation already contains 2.5 mmol $NH_4^+$ ions (i.e. 0.033M), thus total concentration of $NH_4^+$ ions is given as:

$[NH_4^+] = 0.033 + y$

As $y$ is small, $[NH_4OH] \approx 0.033 M$ and $[NH_4^+] \approx 0.033M$. We know,

$K_b = [NH_4^+][OH^-] / [NH_4OH]$

$= y (0.033) / (0.033) = 1.77 \times 10^{-5} M$

Thus, $y = 1.77 \times 10^{-5} = [OH^-]$

$[H^+] = 10^{-14} / 1.77 \times 10^{-5} = 0.56 \times 10^{-9}$

Hence, $pH = 9.24$
