EQUILIBRIUM
The total pressure at equilibrium was found to be 9.15 bar. Calculate $K_c$, $K_p$ and partial pressure at equilibrium.

**Solution**
We know $pV = nRT$
Total volume ($V$) = 1 L
Molecular mass of $N_2O_4$ = 92 g
Number of moles of the gas ($n$) = $\frac{13.8 \text{ g}}{92 \text{ g}} = 0.15 \text{ mol}$
Gas constant ($R$) = $0.083 \text{ bar L mol}^{-1} \text{K}^{-1}$
Temperature ($T$) = 400 K

$pV = nRT$
$p \times 1 \text{ L} = 0.15 \text{ mol} \times 0.083 \text{ bar L mol}^{-1} \text{K}^{-1} \times 400 \text{ K}$
$p = 4.98 \text{ bar}$

| | $N_2O_4$ | $2NO_2$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Initial pressure: | 4.98 bar | 0 |
| At equilibrium: | $(4.98 - x) \text{ bar}$ | $2x \text{ bar}$ |

Hence,
$p_{\text{total}}$ at equilibrium = $p_{N_2O_4} + p_{NO_2}$
