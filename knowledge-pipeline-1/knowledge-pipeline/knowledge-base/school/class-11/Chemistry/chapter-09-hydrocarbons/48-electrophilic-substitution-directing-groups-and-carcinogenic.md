### 9.6 CARCINOGENICITY AND TOXICITY

Benzene and polynuclear hydrocarbons containing more than two benzene rings fused together are toxic and said to possess cancer-producing (carcinogenic) property. Such polynuclear hydrocarbons are formed on incomplete combustion of organic materials like tobacco, coal, and petroleum.
