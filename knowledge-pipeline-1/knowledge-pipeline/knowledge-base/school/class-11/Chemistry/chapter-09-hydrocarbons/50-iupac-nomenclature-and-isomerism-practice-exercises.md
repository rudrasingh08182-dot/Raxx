9.2 Write IUPAC names of the following compounds:
(a) $CH_3CH=C(CH_3)_2$
(b) $CH_2=CH-C≡C-CH_3$
(c) $CH_2=CH-CH_2-CH_2-CH=CH_2$
(d) $CH_2=C(CH_3)_2$
(e) $CH_3-C≡C-CH(CH_3)_2$
(f) $CH_3(CH_2)_4-CH(CH_2)_3CH_3$ attached to $CH_2-CH(CH_3)_2$
(g) $CH_3-CH=CH-CH_2-CH=CH-CH(C_2H_5)-CH_2-CH=CH_2$
