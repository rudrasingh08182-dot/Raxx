If we use enthalpy of bond formation, $(\Delta_f H_{bond})$, which is the enthalpy change when one mole of a particular type of bond is formed from gaseous atoms, then:

$\Delta_f H^0 = \sum \Delta_f H^0_{bonds\ of\ products} - \sum \Delta_f H^0_{bonds\ of\ reactants}$

* Note that the symbol used for bond dissociation enthalpy and mean bond enthalpy is the same.
