Enthalpies of reactions for 
$M(g) \rightarrow M^+(g) + e^-$ (for ionization)
$M(g) + e^- \rightarrow M^-(g)$ (for electron gain)
at temperature, $T$ is 
$\Delta_r H^\circ(T) = \Delta_r H^\circ(0) + \int_0^T \Delta_r C_p^\circ dT$

The value of $C_p$ for each species in the above reaction is $5/2 R$ ($C_V = 3/2 R$).
So, $\Delta_r C_p^\circ = + 5/2 R$ (for ionization)
$\Delta_r C_p^\circ = - 5/2 R$ (for electron gain)

Therefore, 
$\Delta_r H^\circ$ (ionization enthalpy) $= E_0$ (ionization energy) $+ 5/2 RT$
$\Delta_r H^\circ$ (electron gain enthalpy) $= - A$ (electron affinity) $- 5/2 RT$
