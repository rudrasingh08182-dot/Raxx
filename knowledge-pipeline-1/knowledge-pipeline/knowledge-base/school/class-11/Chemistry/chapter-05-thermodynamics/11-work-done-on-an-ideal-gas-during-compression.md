Total volume of the gas is $V_i$ and pressure of the gas inside is $p$. If external pressure is $p_{ex}$ which is greater than $p$, piston is moved inward till the pressure. Fig. 5.5 (a) Work done on an ideal gas in a cylinder when it is compressed by a constant external pressure, $p_{ex}$ (in single step) is equal to the shaded area.
