Given the enthalpy of fusion of ice is $6.00 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$ and heat capacity of water is $4.2 \text{ J/g°C}$.

The change takes place as follows:

Step - 1: $1 \text{ mol H}_2\text{O (l, 100°C)} \rightarrow 1 \text{ mol (l, 0°C)}$ Enthalpy change $\Delta H_1$

Step - 2: $1 \text{ mol H}_2\text{O (l, 0°C)} \rightarrow 1 \text{ mol H}_2\text{O (s, 0°C)}$ Enthalpy change $\Delta H_2$

Total enthalpy change will be:
$\Delta H = \Delta H_1 + \Delta H_2$

$\Delta H_1 = -(18 \times 4.2 \times 100) \text{ J mol}^{-1} = -7560 \text{ J mol}^{-1} = -7.56 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$

$\Delta H_2 = -6.00 \text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$
