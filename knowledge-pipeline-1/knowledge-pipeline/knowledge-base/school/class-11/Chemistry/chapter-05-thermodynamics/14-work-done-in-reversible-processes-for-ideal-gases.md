Processes other than reversible processes are known as irreversible processes. In chemistry, we face problems that can be solved if we relate the work term to the internal pressure of the system. We can relate work to internal pressure of the system under reversible conditions by writing equation 5.3 as follows:

$w_{rev} = -\int_{V_i}^{V_f} p_{in} dV$

Since $dp \times dV$ is very small we can write

$w_{rev} = -\int_{V_i}^{V_f} p_{in} dV$ (5.4)

Now, the pressure of the gas ($p_{in}$ which we can write as $p$ now) can be expressed in terms of its volume through gas equation. For $n$ mol of an ideal gas i.e., $pV = nRT$

$p = \frac{nRT}{V}$
