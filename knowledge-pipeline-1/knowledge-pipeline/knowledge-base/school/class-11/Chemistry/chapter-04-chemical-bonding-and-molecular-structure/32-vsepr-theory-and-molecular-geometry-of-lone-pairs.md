Due to lp-bp (which is more than bp-bp repulsion) the angle between bond pairs is reduced to 107° from 109.5°. The shape should have been tetrahedral if there were all bp but two lp are present so the shape is distorted tetrahedral or angular. The reason is lp-lp repulsion is more than lp-bp repulsion which is more than bp-bp repulsion. Thus, the angle is reduced to 104.5° from 109.5°.
