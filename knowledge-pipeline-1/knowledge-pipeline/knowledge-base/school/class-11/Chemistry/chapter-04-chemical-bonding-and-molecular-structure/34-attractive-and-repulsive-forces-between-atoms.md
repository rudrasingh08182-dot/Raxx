When the two atoms are at large distance from each other, there is no interaction between them. As these two atoms approach each other, new attractive and repulsive forces begin to operate. Attractive forces arise between: (i) nucleus of one atom and its own electron that is $N_A – e_A$ and $N_B – e_B$.
