### Problem 8.9
Derive the structure of (i) 3-chlorohexane, (ii) pent-2-en-1-ol, (iii) 4-nitrocyclohexene, (iv) cyclohex-2-en-1-ol, (v) 3-hydroxyheptanal.

### Solution
(i) 'hexane' indicates the presence of 6 carbon atoms in the chain. The functional group chloro is present at carbon 3. Hence, the structure of the compound is $CH_3CH_2CH(Cl)CH_2CH_2CH_3$.

(ii) 'pent' indicates that parent hydrocarbon contains 5 carbon atoms in the chain. 'en' and 'ol' correspond to the functional groups C=C and -OH at carbon atoms 2 and 1 respectively. Thus, the structure is $CH_3CH=CHCH_2CH_2OH$.

(iii) Six membered ring containing a carbon-carbon double bond is implied by cyclohexene, which is numbered as shown in (i). The prefix 4-nitro means that a nitro group is present on C4. Thus, complete structural formula of the compound is (ii). Double bond is suffixed functional group whereas $-NO_2$ is prefixed functional group therefore double bond gets preference over $-NO_2$ group.

(iv) '1-ol' means that a -OH group is present at C1. -OH is suffixed functional group and gets preference over C=C bond. Thus the structure is as shown in (ii).

(v) 'heptanal' indicates the compound to be an aldehyde containing 7 carbon atoms in the parent chain. The '3-hydroxy' indicates that -OH group is present at carbon 3. Thus, the structural formula of the compound is $CH_3CH_2CH(OH)CH_2CH_2CH_2CHO$. Carbon atom of –CHO group is included while numbering the carbon chain.
