### Estimation of Nitrogen

Percentage of nitrogen = $\frac{28}{22400} \times \frac{V}{m} \times 100$

**Problem 8.21**
In Dumas’ method for estimation of nitrogen, 0.3 g of an organic compound gave 41.9 mL of nitrogen collected at 300 K temperature and 715 mm pressure. Calculate the percentage composition of nitrogen in the compound (aqueous tension at 300 K = 15 mm).

**Solution**
Volume of nitrogen collected at 300 K and 715 mm pressure is 41.9 mL.
Actual pressure = $715 - 15 = 700$ mm.

Volume of nitrogen at STP = $\frac{273 \times 700 \times 41.9}{300 \times 760} = 41.9$ mL

22400 mL of $N_2$ at STP weighs 28 g.
41.9 mL of nitrogen weighs = $\frac{28 \times 41.9}{22400}$ g

Percentage of nitrogen = $\frac{28 \times 41.9 \times 100}{22400 \times 0.3} = 17.46\%$
