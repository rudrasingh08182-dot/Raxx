To calculate the volume of nitrogen at STP (let it be $V_1$ mL), we use the formula:

$\frac{P_1 V_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2 V_2}{T_2}$

Here, $P_1$ and $V_1$ are the pressure and volume of nitrogen. $P_1$ is different from the atmospheric pressure at which nitrogen gas is collected. The value of $P_1$ is obtained by the relation:

$P_1 = \text{Atmospheric pressure} - \text{Aqueous tension}$

$V_1$ mL of $N_2$ at STP weighs $1.25 \times 10^{-3}$ g.
