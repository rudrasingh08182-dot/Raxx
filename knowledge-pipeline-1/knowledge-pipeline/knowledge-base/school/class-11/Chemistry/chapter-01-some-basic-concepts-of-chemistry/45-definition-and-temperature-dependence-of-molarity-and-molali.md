Since molarity (M) note that molarity of a solution depends upon temperature because volume of a solution is temperature dependent.

4. Molality
It is defined as the number of moles of solute present in 1 kg of solvent. It is denoted by m. Thus, Molality (m) = $\frac{\text{No. of moles of solute}}{\text{Mass of solvent in kg}}$
