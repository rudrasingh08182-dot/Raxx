### Unit Conversion and Dimensional Analysis

We know that $1 \text{ in} = 2.54 \text{ cm}$. From this equivalence, we can write:

$\frac{2.54 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ in}} = 1$ and $\frac{1 \text{ in}}{2.54 \text{ cm}} = 1$

Both of these are called unit factors. If some number is multiplied by these unit factors (i.e., 1), it will not be affected otherwise.

Say, the $3 \text{ in}$ given above is multiplied by the unit factor. So,

$3 \text{ in} = 3 \text{ in} \times \frac{2.54 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ in}} = 3 \times 2.54 \text{ cm} = 7.62 \text{ cm}$

Now, the unit factor by which multiplication is to be done is that unit factor ($\frac{2.54 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ in}}$ in the above case) which gives the desired units i.e., the numerator should have that part which is required in the desired result.

It should also be noted in the above example that units can be handled just like other numerical part. It can be cancelled, divided, multiplied, squared, etc. Let us study one more example.

**Example:** A jug contains 2 L of milk. Calculate the volume of the milk in $\text{m}^3$.

**Solution:**
Since $1 \text{ L} = 1000 \text{ cm}^3$ and $1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm}$, which gives:

$\frac{1 \text{ m}}{100 \text{ cm}} = 1$ and $\frac{100 \text{ cm}}{1 \text{ m}} = 1$

To get $\text{m}^3$ from the above unit factors, the first unit factor is taken and it is cubed:

$(\frac{1 \text{ m}}{100 \text{ cm}})^3 = \frac{1 \text{ m}^3}{10^6 \text{ cm}^3} = 1$

Now $2 \text{ L} = 2 \times 1000 \text{ cm}^3$. The above is multiplied by the unit factor:

$2 \times 1000 \text{ cm}^3 \times \frac{1 \text{ m}^3}{10^6 \text{ cm}^3} = 2 \times 10^{-3} \text{ m}^3$

**Example:** How many seconds are there in 2 days?

**Solution:**
Here, we know $1 \text{ day} = 24 \text{ hours (h)}$ or $\frac{1 \text{ day}}{24 \text{ h}} = 1$ and $\frac{24 \text{ h}}{1 \text{ day}} = 1$

Then, $1 \text{ h} = 60 \text{ min}$ or $\frac{1 \text{ h}}{60 \text{ min}} = 1$ and $\frac{60 \text{ min}}{1 \text{ h}} = 1$

So, for converting 2 days to seconds:

The unit factors can be multiplied in series in one step only as follows:

$2 \text{ days} \times \frac{24 \text{ h}}{1 \text{ day}} \times \frac{60 \text{ min}}{1 \text{ h}} \times \frac{60 \text{ s}}{1 \text{ min}} = 2 \times 24 \times 60 \times 60 \text{ s} = 172800 \text{ s}$
