**Step 4.** Write down the empirical formula by mentioning the numbers after writing the symbols of respective elements. $CH_2Cl$ is, thus, the empirical formula of the above compound.

**Step 5.** Writing molecular formula:
(a) Determine empirical formula mass by adding the atomic masses of various atoms present in the empirical formula. For $CH_2Cl$, empirical formula mass is $12.01 + (2 \times 1.008) + 35.453 = 49.48 \text{ g}$.
(b) Divide Molar mass by empirical formula mass: $\frac{Molar mass}{Empirical formula mass} = 2 = (n)$.
(c) Multiply empirical formula by $n$ obtained above to get the molecular formula. Empirical formula = $CH_2Cl$, $n = 2$. Hence, molecular formula is $C_2H_4Cl_2$.
